==============================================
CZ Deferrals and Accruals (časové rozlišení)
==============================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Points Odoo's deferral tools at the Czech accounts 381, 383, 384 and 385 — the OCA cut-offs on Community, Odoo's own deferrals on Enterprise.**

What it is for
================

*Časové rozlišení* moves a cost or revenue into the period it belongs to when the invoice falls into another one: insurance paid in advance for next year, rent received in advance, a service of December invoiced in January. The Czech chart keeps four accounts for it:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Account
     - Czech term
     - Gloss
     - Typical case
   * - 381
     - Náklady příštích období
     - prepaid expense
     - insurance or rent paid in advance
   * - 383
     - Výdaje příštích období
     - accrued expense
     - rent paid in arrears
   * - 384
     - Výnosy příštích období
     - deferred revenue
     - rent or a subscription received in advance
   * - 385
     - Příjmy příštích období
     - accrued revenue
     - rent received in arrears

Odoo has two different engines for this, depending on the edition. Neither knows the Czech accounts. Two small modules connect them:

- **This module** (``l10n_cz_account_cutoff``) fills the default accounts of the **OCA cut-offs** (``account_cutoff_start_end_dates``), which run on Community and Enterprise — see `Cut-offs <#cutoffs>`__.

- ``l10n_cz_account_deferred`` fills the accounts of **Odoo Enterprise's own deferrals** — see `Enterprise deferrals <#enterprise>`__.

Both install themselves when their engine and the Czech chart (``l10n_cz``) are present. Neither posts anything on its own: they only make the engine post to the right accounts.

Items whose amount is **not yet known** at the period end belong on the estimated-item accounts 388 and 389 (*dohadné účty*), not here; see ``l10n_cssk_accrual``.

Legal background
==================

- Under `§ 3 odst. 1 of the Accounting Act <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1991/563>`__ items are booked in the period they relate to in time and substance, and all costs and revenues regardless of when they are paid or received.

- `Vyhláška 500/2002 Sb. <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2002/500>`__, § 13 and § 19, describes the items: *náklady příštích období* are expenditure relating to costs of future periods, *příjmy příštích období* amounts not yet received that relate to revenue of the current period and are not booked directly as receivables, *výdaje příštích období* costs of the current period not yet paid, and *výnosy příštích období* receipts that belong to revenue of future periods. Both paragraphs set the test for using these accounts: at the time of booking, the purpose, the **amount** and the period must all be known. An item whose amount is not known goes on a *dohadný účet*.

Cut-offs (OCA engine)
=======================

What the module sets
----------------------

On a company with the Czech chart — when the chart is loaded, or on install for a company that already has it — the module fills, only where empty:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Setting (*Accounting → Configuration → Settings*, block *Cut-offs*)
     - Account
   * - Default Account for Prepaid Expense
     - 381000
   * - Default Account for Accrued Expenses
     - 383000
   * - Default Account for Prepaid Revenue
     - 384000
   * - Default Account for Accrued Revenues
     - 385000
   * - Default Cut-off Journal
     - the company's first *Miscellaneous* journal

It also switches **Accrual On Taxes** off. The OCA engine has it on by default and then accrues the VAT of the source lines as well, on a *Default Tax Account for Accrued Expense / Revenue* the Czech chart does not have, so every cut-off over taxed lines stopped with *Missing 'Accrued Expense Tax Account'*. Nor would it be right: input VAT cannot be deducted before the tax document is in hand (`§ 73 odst. 1 písm. a) of the VAT Act <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235#p73>`__), so an accrual on 383 / 385 is the amount without VAT. On install and upgrade the option is switched off only where no accrued-tax account is configured, on the company or on a tax; if your accountant set one up, it stays on.

You can change any of them there. Review two more settings of the same block, which this module leaves alone:

- **Post Cut-off Journal Entry** is off: the cut-off entry is created as a draft for you to check and post.

- **Partner on Journal Items by Default** controls whether the entry keeps the partner on each line.

Entering the dates
--------------------

The cut-off engine works from **start and end dates on invoice and bill lines** (*Start Date*, *End Date*). Enter them on every line whose cost or revenue covers a period — for example 1. 7. 2026 – 30. 6. 2027 on an annual insurance bill. To make the dates compulsory for a product, tick *Must Have Start/End Dates* on it.

Running a cut-off
-------------------

At the period end: *Accounting → Accounting → Cut-offs*, then the type, then *New*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Menu
     - Account
     - Lines it picks up
   * - Prepaid Expense
     - 381
     - bill lines dated on or before the cut-off date whose end date is after it; the part after the cut-off date is deferred
   * - Prepaid Revenue
     - 384
     - the same for invoice lines
   * - Accrued Expense
     - 383
     - bill lines dated **after** the cut-off date whose start date is on or before it; the part up to the cut-off date is brought back
   * - Accrued Revenue
     - 385
     - the same for invoice lines

The amount is split by days. Enter the cut-off date and check the source journals, then click **Re-Generate Lines**, review the lines and click **Create Journal Entry**. The entry is dated on the cut-off date.

Two practical consequences:

- 383 and 385 work only with invoices **already booked** with a later date — run them when the January invoices for December services are in, before the books are closed.

- The cut-off entry is **not reversed automatically**. Reverse it yourself as of the first day of the next period (*Reverse Entry* on the entry), so the original invoice lines carry the cost again in the period they belong to.

Only one cut-off per type and date can exist in a company.

Enterprise deferrals
======================

Odoo Enterprise (``account_accountant``) has its own deferrals: on a bill or invoice line you set the period in the **Deferred Date** column, and Odoo spreads the amount through a deferral account by month or by day.

``l10n_cz_account_deferred`` installs itself with ``account_accountant`` on a database with the Czech chart and sets, in *Accounting → Configuration → Settings*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Setting
     - Value
   * - Deferred expense entries → Deferred expense
     - 381000
   * - Deferred revenue entries → Deferred revenue
     - 384000
   * - *Journal* of both blocks
     - the company's first *Miscellaneous* journal, if empty

Without it Enterprise fills the two accounts with the first current-asset and the first current-liability account of the chart, whatever they are. On install the module sets both accounts on every Czech company where they are empty or still on that generic choice; an account you had set by hand is kept. Later, only new companies are touched.

Whether entries are generated when the bill is posted (*On bill validation*) or in one batch (*Manually & Grouped*), and whether the amount is split by months or days, are Enterprise's own settings in the same blocks. The deferral reports are under *Accounting → Review → Regularization Entries → Deferred Expenses / Deferred Revenues*.

Enterprise deferrals cover only the in-advance cases, 381 and 384. Accrued items on 383 and 385 need the cut-offs above or a manual entry.

Limitations
=============

- Both modules only set default accounts and journals. They neither compute nor post anything themselves.

- Accrued items are booked without VAT. For a company that is not a VAT payer, whose VAT is a cost, add the VAT to the accrual by hand.

- Cut-off entries are not reversed automatically.

- On Enterprise both engines can be installed at the same time, and an invoice line then shows both sets of dates. Use one engine for a given line: dates entered in both would defer the amount twice.

- Account 382 *Komplexní náklady příštích období* is not mapped.

- Neither module checks the vyhláška's condition that the amount and period are known; an estimate does not belong in a cut-off.
