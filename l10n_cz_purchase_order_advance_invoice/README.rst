============================================
Received Advance Invoices — Czech Accounts
============================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Wires the received-advance settings to the Czech chart of accounts.**

Fills in the received-advance settings for every company with the Czech chart of accounts: it creates the reconcilable payable clearing account 314001 *Poskytnuté zálohy – zúčtování (daňový doklad)* (in English *Advances provided – clearing (tax document)*), uses 314000 *Poskytnuté provozní zálohy* for paid advances, and creates the purchase journal PDADV *Daňové doklady k odeslaným platbám*. No long-term account is set.

It runs at installation for the companies that already have the Czech chart, and again whenever the chart is loaded later, for example for a new company. It fills only settings that are still empty.
