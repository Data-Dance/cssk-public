| Party | Social | Health | **Total** | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The single most important 2026 change for net pay: **employee health insurance rose
from 4% to 5%** (ZŤP 2% → 2.5%). Source: <https://www.dovera.sk/platitel/zamestnavatel/tema-legislativne-informacie/odvody-od-januara-2026>

## 1. Social insurance (Sociálne poistenie)

Rates are stable across 2025 and 2026; only the assessment-base ceiling changed.

### 1.1 Sub-fund breakdown

| Fund (SK / EN) | Employee | Employer | Max base applies? | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nemocenské (sickness) | 1.4% | 1.4% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Starobné (old-age pension) | 4% | 14% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Invalidné (disability) | 3% | 3% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Poistenie v nezamestnanosti (unemployment) | 1% | 0.5% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Financovanie podpory v čase skrátenej práce (short-time work) | — | 0.5% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Garančné poistenie (guarantee) | — | 0.25% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| Úrazové poistenie (work accident) | — | 0.8% | **NO ceiling** | [2025 & 2026] |
| Rezervný fond solidarity (reserve fund) | — | 4.75% | yes | [2025 & 2026] |
| **TOTAL SOCIAL** | **9.4%** | **25.2%** | | |

Notes:
- Employer unemployment is split **0.5% unemployment + 0.5% short-time-work financing**
  (the old combined 1% employer unemployment was split in 2023). Together = 1%.
- **Úrazové (accident) 0.8% has NO maximum assessment base** — it is charged on the
  full gross. All other funds are capped at the monthly maximum base.
- Disability (invalidné) is **not** paid by/for old-age or early-retirement pensioners.
- Unemployment is not paid by/for persons drawing old-age/disability pension.

Source (official table, effective 1 Jan 2026):
<https://www.socpoist.sk/socialne-poistenie/platenie-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho-od-1-6>
and <https://www.podnikajte.sk/socialne-a-zdravotne-odvody/odvody-zamestnanca-zamestnavatela-od-2026>

### 1.2 Maximum monthly assessment base (maximálny vymeriavací základ)

| Year | Max monthly base | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | **€15,730** | 11 × 1/12 of general base (avg wage 2023 ≈ €1,430) | <https://www.socpoist.sk/socialne-poistenie/platenie-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho-od-1-5> |
| 2026 | **€16,764** | 11 × €1,524 | <https://www.socpoist.sk/news/nove-vymeriavacie-zaklady-pre-platenie-poistneho-od-1-januara-2026> |

Note: the multiplier was raised **from 7× to 11×** effective 1 Jan 2025 — this is why
the ceiling jumped so sharply vs earlier years.
Source: <https://www.podnikajte.sk/socialne-a-zdravotne-odvody/odvody-szco-od-1-1-2025>

At the 2026 ceiling (€16,764) the monthly social premium is **€1,575.81 employee /
€4,090.41 employer**.
Source: <https://www.socpoist.sk/socialne-poistenie/platenie-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho/tabulky-platenia-poistneho-od-1-6>

### 1.3 Minimum assessment base
For **employees** there is **no** minimum social-insurance assessment base (removed 2010).
The minimum base (€914.40 for 2026) applies only to self-employed (SZČO) and voluntarily
insured persons. Source: (socpoist table, as above).

## 2. Health insurance (Zdravotné poistenie)

| Category | Employee | Employer | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | **4%** | **11%** | [2025] |
| Standard | **5%** | **11%** | **[2026 confirmed]** |
| ZŤP / disabled (osoba so zdravotným postihnutím) | 2% | 5.5% | [2025] |
| ZŤP / disabled | **2.5%** | 5.5% | **[2026 confirmed]** |

- **No maximum assessment base** for health insurance (charged on full gross).
- **No minimum base for employees on the individual level**, BUT total health premium
  (employee + employer) must be at least computed from the **subsistence minimum
  (životné minimum)**. If the wage-based premium falls short, the difference is topped up
  (doplatok): min životné minimum = **€273.99 (2025)** / **€284.13 (2026)**. In 2026 the
  employee minimum monthly advance is **€14.21** and employer **€31.25**; combined minimum
  **€45.45**. Source: <https://www.vszp.sk/platitelia/platenie-poistneho/oznamenia-zmeny/zmeny-od-01-01.2026/>,
  <https://www.superpoistenie.sk/aktuality/kolko-sa-plati-zdravotne-poistenie>
- **ZŤP treatment**: employees with ≥50% functional impairment pay the halved rate from
  the first calendar month in which disability is recognised.
- **Odvodová odpočítateľná položka (OOP) on health** — a deductible item reducing the
  employee's health assessment base for low earners: max **€380/month [2025]**, phased out
  as income rises (fully gone above ~€570). **2026 value TBD** (not re-confirmed here — flag
  before relying on it). Source: <https://www.epravo.sk/top/clanky/minimalne-zdravotne-odvody-v-roku-2025-6476.html>
- **Ročné zúčtovanie zdravotného poistenia (RZZP)**: an annual health-insurance
  reconciliation performed by the health insurer (not the employer's monthly payroll) that
  trues up advances vs actual annual income; can create an additional levy or a refund.
  Relevant to year-end, not the monthly payslip.

Source (rates): <https://www.podnikajte.sk/socialne-a-zdravotne-odvody/odvody-zamestnanca-zamestnavatela-od-2026>

## 3. Income tax on employment (Daň z príjmov zo závislej činnosti)

### 3.1 Tax base
Monthly tax base = **gross wage − employee social insurance − employee health insurance
− monthly non-taxable part (NČZD)**. The advance tax is computed on that base, **not** on
gross. (This is the single most common modelling error.)

### 3.2 Rates / bands

**2025 — two bands** (25% threshold = 176.8× životné minimum):

| Band | Annual tax base | Monthly equivalent | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ≤ €47,537.98 | ≤ €3,961.50 | 19% |
| 2 | > €47,537.98 | > €3,961.50 | 25% |

**2026 — four bands (progressive reform, III. konsolidácia)** — **[2026 confirmed]**:

| Band | Annual tax base | Monthly equivalent | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ≤ €43,983.32 | ≤ €3,665.28 | 19% |
| 2 | €43,983.32 – €60,349.21 | €3,665.28 – €5,029.10 | 25% |
| 3 | €60,349.21 – €75,010.32 | up to ~€6,250.86 | 30% |
| 4 | > €75,010.32 | above | 35% |

The 25% threshold was **lowered from 176.8× to 154.8× životné minimum**; new **30%
(212.4×)** and **35% (264.0×)** bands were added.
Source: Finančná správa novela 34/DZPaU/2025/I
<https://www.financnasprava.sk/_img/pfsedit/Dokumenty_PFS/Zverejnovanie_dok/Dane/Novinky_leg/Priame_dane_uct/2025/2025.10.29_034_DZPaU_2025_I_Novel_ZDP.pdf>,
<https://www.podnikajte.sk/dan-z-prijmov/progresivne-zdanenie-prijmov-fyzickych-osob-od-2026>

### 3.3 Non-taxable part per taxpayer (nezdaniteľná časť základu dane na daňovníka, NČZD)

| Year | Monthly NČZD | Annual NČZD | Full amount if annual tax base ≤ | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | **€479.48** | €5,753.79 | €25,426.27 (92.8× ŽM) | <https://www.podnikajte.sk/dan-z-prijmov/nezdanitelne-casti-zakladu-dane-2025> |
| 2026 | **€497.23** | €5,966.73 | €26,083.13 (91.8× ŽM) | <https://www.podnikajte.sk/dan-z-prijmov/nezdanitelne-casti-zakladu-dane-2026> |

Above the threshold, NČZD tapers by formula (44.2× ŽM − ¼ × tax base) and reaches €0 for
high earners. The 2026 multiplier for the taper start dropped from 92.8× to 91.8×, i.e. it
phases out slightly faster. Monthly NČZD is applied only if the employee has signed the
*Vyhlásenie* (declaration) with the employer.
Source: <https://jaspis.sk/aktuality/nezdanitelna-cast-zakladu-dane>

### 3.4 Child tax bonus (daňový bonus na dieťa)

| Age tier | Monthly max | Year |
|---|---|---|
| child < 15 years | **€100** | [2025 & 2026] |
| 15 to < 18 years | **€50** | [2025 & 2026] |
| ≥ 18 years | €0 (entitlement ends) | [2025 & 2026] |

**Percentage-of-tax-base cap** (the bonus may not exceed this % of the partial tax base),
by number of children — **[2025 & 2026]**:

| # children | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| % of base | 29% | 36% | 43% | 50% | 57% | 64% |

**High-income taper**: for 2026, if the annual tax base exceeds **€27,432** the bonus is
reduced by 1/10 of the excess per child (2025 threshold was lower). Source:
<https://www.podnikajte.sk/dan-z-prijmov/danovy-bonus-na-dieta-2026>,
<https://podpora.financnasprava.sk/272586-Da%C5%88ov%C3%BD-bonus-na-die%C5%A5a-v-roku-2025>

The tax bonus is a **credit against computed tax** (can reduce tax below zero → paid out),
not a deduction from the base.

### 3.5 Employee premium / zamestnanecká prémia
A separate low-income full-year relief (zamestnanecká prémia) exists but is rarely payable
in practice (tight eligibility, applied in annual reconciliation, not monthly). Note it for
completeness only. Source: <https://www.relia.sk/Article.aspx?ID=1314>

### 3.6 Monthly advance computation (summary)
1. base = gross − employee social (9.4%, capped) − employee health (5% 2026 / 4% 2025, uncapped)
2. base −= monthly NČZD (if declaration signed and eligible)
3. tax = 19% up to monthly band-1 ceiling, 25% (then 30/35% in 2026) above
4. tax −= child tax bonus (subject to age/percentage/taper caps)
5. tax −= any employee tax bonus for mortgage interest, if claimed

## 5. Meal allowance / meal vouchers (stravné / gazdovské / gastrolístky)

Employer **obligation**: for a shift of 5–12 hours the employer must provide meals and
contribute **at least 55%** of the meal price. Since 1 Jan 2023 the employee may choose
between a meal voucher (gastrolístok), an on-site meal, or a **financial contribution**
(finančný príspevok) paid with the wage.

| Item | Value | Year | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stravné (business trip 5–12h) | €8.30 (Apr) → €8.80 → **€9.30 (from 1 Dec 2025)** | 2025 | <https://www.podnikajte.sk/stravne-a-pracovne-cesty/stravne-2025-tabulka> |
| Stravné (5–12h) | **€9.30** | 2026 | <https://www.podnikajte.sk/stravne-a-pracovne-cesty/stravne-2026-tabulka> |
| Min voucher (gastrolístok) value = 75% of stravné | **€6.98** | 2026 | <https://www.bezucto.sk/blog/stravne-2026-zamestnavatel> |
| Employer contribution — min (55% of voucher min) | **€3.84** | 2026 | as above |
| Employer contribution — max tax-deductible (55% of €9.30) | **€5.12** | 2026 | as above |

Tax treatment: the employer's contribution up to 55% of stravné (max €5.12 in 2026) is a
tax-deductible cost and is **exempt from employee income tax and from the assessment base
for both social and health insurance**. The financial contribution is paid in full with the
wage but does **not** enter the tax base or the contribution base.
Source: <https://www.finsider.sk/servis/stravne-2026/>

## 6. Agreements outside employment (dohody: DoVP / DoPČ)

| Type | SK | Hour limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| DoVP | Dohoda o vykonaní práce | max **350 h / calendar year** per employer | <https://www.podnikajte.sk/pracovne-pravo-bozp> / danovy-poradca.sk/blog/dohody-2026 |
| DoPČ | Dohoda o pracovnej činnosti | max **10 h / week** (average) | as above |

Contribution treatment (2026):
- **Regular-income dohodári**: pay **full social + health insurance**, both employee and
  employer, like an employment relationship.
- **Pensioners on a dohoda**: **no health insurance**; social insurance may be reduced.
- **Students & pensioners (designated "OOP" agreement)**: **odvodová odpočítateľná
  položka = €200/month** reduces the **pension-insurance** assessment base to zero up to
  €200; above €200 pension is paid on the excess. OOP does **not** reduce accident or
  guarantee insurance. Only one such agreement at a time.
- **Seasonal work (DoPČ na sezónnu prácu)**: a **mandatory** special OOP of **€762/month
  (2026)** applies.

Source: <https://www.socpoist.sk/socialne-poistenie/platenie-poistneho/poistne-pri-dohodaroch-zamestnavatelzamestnanec>,
<https://www.danovy-poradca.sk/blog/dohody-2026>,
<https://www.kros.sk/blog/zamestnavanie-na-dohodu-2026-limity-odvody-a-najcastejsie-chyby/>

## 7. 13th / 14th salary
As of 2024 the special payroll-tax/contribution exemptions for the 13th and 14th salary
were **abolished**; such payments are ordinary taxable wage subject to full tax and
contributions. No special monthly-payroll handling is required beyond treating them as
regular gross. Source: <https://www.relia.sk/Article.aspx?ID=1314>

## 8. Employer total-cost illustration (2026, no meal allowance)

Gross €2,000 → employer add-on 36.2% = **€724** (social €504 + health €220) → total
employer cost **€2,724** (+ meal allowance obligation). Employee keeps gross − 14.4%
(€288) − income tax (on base after NČZD and contributions).
