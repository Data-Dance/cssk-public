Slovakia - Historical social assessment and premiums
======================================================================

Reviewed 2027 historical assessment-base register and standard social
premiums. This is a separate foundation for post-insurance income under
section 139b, not a completed ZECN payroll or VPP implementation.

It provides an Odoo register, exact month/fund allocation, separately reviewed
standard social premiums, audit attachments and tests. Historical insurance
coverage, deductions, effective ceilings and payment-at-ceiling facts must be
supplied and reviewed explicitly. It does not infer eligibility from pension
type alone. See ``doc/en.md`` for scope, sources and input schema.

No health insurance, income tax, payslip, accounting entry or official filing
is created. Existing payroll/reporting behavior is not overridden.
