======================================================
SK Social Insurance Registration of Employees (RLFO)
======================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Registrations (prihláška) and de-registrations (odhláška) of employees with the Sociálna poisťovňa, entered as a batch of events and exported as XML checked against the official schema.**

What it is for
================

Before an employee starts work, the employer registers them with the **Sociálna poisťovňa**, and it de-registers them when the insurance ends. The form is the *Registračný list fyzickej osoby* (**RLFO**); this module produces its electronic batch for employees (the *RLZEC* schema), one file with any number of registrations and de-registrations.

Unlike the contribution statements, the RLFO is **not built from payslips**. You enter the events yourself; the module fills in the relationship type and the employee's personal data, renders the XML and validates it. It uses ``l10n_cssk_payroll_declaration_base`` only for the export, validation and filed-copy handling, so it does not need a payroll engine — but the relationship type is only proposed correctly when one of the Slovak payroll modules is installed (see the Slovak payroll guide).

Legal background
==================

References are to the `Social Insurance Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/>`__.

- **Registration and de-registration.** Under `§ 231 ods. 1 písm. b) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-231.odsek-1.pismeno-b>`__ the employer registers an employee for sickness, pension and unemployment insurance (an irregular-income employee under § 4 ods. 2 for pension insurance only) **before the insurance arises, at the latest before the employee starts work**, and de-registers them **within eight days** of the insurance ending. A registration for an insurance relationship that never arose must be cancelled (*zrušiť prihlásenie*).

- **Interruption.** An interruption of the insurance is reported within eight days — `§ 231 ods. 1 písm. d) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-231.odsek-1.pismeno-d>`__. This module does not produce that event.

- **Statutory owners.** With the registration, the employer reports whether the employee is a statutory body, or a member of one, with **at least 50 % of the employer's property**, and reports any change of that within eight days — `§ 231 ods. 1 písm. o) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-231.odsek-1.pismeno-o>`__. The registration carries it as ``zamSUcastou``.

- The form and the way of sending it are set by the Sociálna poisťovňa — `§ 231 ods. 2 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-231.odsek-2>`__.

Setting it up
===============

- *Company ID* (IČO), 8 to 12 digits, on the company form.

- *Sociálna poisťovňa variable symbol*, exactly 10 digits — fill it in on the RLFO form itself. It is stored on the company and shared with the MVP, VPP and ELDP modules, so it is entered once. Changing it needs the right to edit companies.

- Per employee: the **birth number** in *Identification No* (9 or 10 digits) and the date of birth (*Birthday*). The surname and first name are taken from the employee's name: the **last word is the surname** and everything before it the first name — write employee names as *First name(s) Surname*. With ``hr_employee_firstname`` installed, its separate first and last name fields are used instead.

Preparing a batch
===================

*Payroll Declarations → SK RLFO (registrations)*, then *New*. *Year* and *Month* do not limit the events, but they choose the form vintage: a batch for January 2027 or later is generated in the RLZEC 2027 schema, an earlier one in RLZEC 2026.

Add one line per event under *Registration events*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Column
     - What to enter
   * - *Employee*
     - the employee
   * - *Event type*
     - *Registration* (prihláška, ``PA``) or *De-registration* (odhláška, ``OD``)
   * - *Relationship type (typZec)*
     - proposed from the employee's contract, editable — see below
   * - *Insurance start (datVznikPoist)*
     - required for both event types
   * - *Insurance end (datZaniku)*
     - required for a de-registration
   * - *Statutory owner 50%+*
     - for a registration: *Not reviewed* (the default), *Not a statutory member with at least 50% ownership*, or *Statutory member with at least 50% ownership*
   * - *Ownership evidence*
     - for a registration: where the answer comes from, with its date — the commercial register extract, the shareholders' resolution, or "ordinary employee, no statutory role"

The relationship type is proposed from the contract's *Agreement Type* and *Regular monthly income*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Contract
     - Regular income
     - Irregular income
   * - employment (pracovný pomer)
     - ZEC
     - ZEC
   * - DoVP
     - ZECD1
     - ZECD1N
   * - DoPČ
     - ZECD2
     - ZECD2N
   * - DoBPŠ
     - ZECD3
     - ZECD3N

*ZECN* (employee with an irregular income) can be chosen by hand. All agreement codes, the DoBPŠ ones included, are de-registered through the agreement branch of the schema.

The ownership status is a fact as of the registration, entered for each event. It is not taken from the employee's *Guarantee-insurance exempt* box, which can change later. Once the batch is generated both fields are locked; reset the batch to draft to correct them.

Then **Generate**. The XML is validated against the vintage's schema (``RLZEC-v2026.xsd`` or ``RLZEC-v2027.xsd``) and attached; upload it to the Sociálna poisťovňa portal and click **Mark Submitted**. A de-registration is written in the branch that matches its relationship type (agreement, irregular income, or regular employment).

Generation stops if the batch has no events, if an employee lacks a valid birth number, if a date required for the event is missing, or if a registration's ownership status is *Not reviewed* or has no evidence.

Limitations
=============

- **Two event types only:** registration and de-registration. A change (*zmena*), an interruption (*prerušenie*) or a cancelled registration (*zrušenie prihlásenia*) must be filed through the portal.

- **Nothing is filled in from the contracts but the relationship type.** The dates are entered by hand and are not checked against the contract or the employee's hire and departure dates, and no event is created when an employee is hired or leaves — keeping to the deadlines is up to you.

- The relationship type is proposed when the employee is chosen and is not re-checked later against the insurance actually owed.

- Without separate name fields only the last word is taken as the surname: check a two-word surname such as *Anna Mária Nová Kováčová* in the XML.

- Contact data in the file are the company's phone and e-mail and the name of the user who generates the batch.

- A change of the ownership status after the registration is a *zmena*, which this module does not produce.

- RLZEC 2026 and 2027 are shipped. The 2027 schema's new elements for the agreement OOP of a parent on maternity, paternity or parental leave are not produced. Schema validation checks the shape of the file, not the data.
