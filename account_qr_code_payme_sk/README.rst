==================
payme (Slovakia)
==================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Prints a Slovak payme payment link as a QR code on customer invoices; scanning it opens the customer's banking app with the payment filled in.**

What it is for
================

**payme** is the payment link standard of the Slovak Banking Association (*Slovenská banková asociácia*): a ``payme.sk`` web address that carries the account, amount and payment identification. This module prints that link as a QR code on your invoices. The customer scans it with the phone camera or a banking app, and their banking app opens with the payment filled in.

What the national formats have in common — switching QR codes on, the bank account they need and how Odoo picks a format for each invoice — is described in ``account_qr_code_frame_provider``, installed with this module.

Setting it up
===============

1. Switch on *QR Codes* in *Accounting → Configuration → Settings* — see the common setup.

2. Use a bank account in **IBAN** form from a SEPA country, with an *Account Holder Name*.

3. Invoice in **EUR**. payme is not offered for other currencies.

Odoo picks payme **automatically** for a euro invoice when both your company and the customer are in Slovakia (or the customer's country is not filled in) and PAY by square is not installed. Otherwise pick *payme (Slovensko)* in the invoice's *Payment QR-code* field — see Which format an invoice gets.

What the link contains
========================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Value
     - Taken from
   * - IBAN
     - the invoice's *Recipient Bank* account
   * - Amount, currency
     - the amount still open on the invoice, EUR
   * - Payment identification
     - with ``l10n_cssk_payment_symbols`` installed and symbols on the invoice: ``/VS…/SS…/KS…``; otherwise the *Payment Reference* or the invoice number. At most 35 characters.
   * - Creditor's name
     - *Account Holder Name* of the account, or the name of its owner; at most 70 characters
   * - Message
     - the *Payment Reference*, or the invoice number when there is none

The code is drawn inside the payme frame.

Limitations
=============

- No due date is passed; the banking app proposes its own payment date.

- Euro only.

- With PAY by square also installed, PAY by square is the one Odoo picks automatically; see the common limitations.
