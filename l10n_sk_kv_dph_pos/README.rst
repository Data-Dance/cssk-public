=================================================
SK VAT Control Statement — Point of Sale in D.1
=================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Reports the sales of every Odoo Point of Sale session in oddiel D.1 of the Slovak kontrolný výkaz DPH, as eKasa turnover, instead of D.2.**

What it is for
================

A Slovak VAT payer reports the turnover recorded by its eKasa cash registers in oddiel **D.1** of the kontrolný výkaz DPH, and only what eKasa did not record in D.2. A sale at an Odoo Point of Sale till is such a turnover: the till is a *pokladnica eKasa*, for instance the Finančná správa's virtual cash register through ``pos_vrp2``.

Odoo posts a session's sales as one closing entry with no customer, so the Slovak section rules of ``l10n_sk_kv_dph`` read it as a supply to private individuals and filed it in **D.2**, unless its journal had been marked ``D.1`` by hand. With this module installed every session's closing entry goes to **D.1**.

Legal background
==================

- The `VAT act (zákon o DPH) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/>`__, § 78a: the kontrolný výkaz.

- The poučenie to the kontrolný výkaz, oddiel D.1: the data are reported by the payer "ktorý je povinný evidovať tržby pokladnicou e-kasa klient", and D.2 takes "len tie dodania tovaru alebo služby, ktoré nezahrnul do D.1".

- The act on the recording of sales (zákon č. 384/2025 Z. z., before 2026 zákon č. 289/2008 Z. z.) obliges the seller to record every sale at the point of sale in a pokladnica eKasa.

How it works
==============

Nothing to set up: the module installs itself when both the Slovak control statement and Point of Sale are installed.

- The **closing entry of a POS session** is reported in D.1: the turnover including VAT and the base and tax at the basic and the reduced rates (see the D.1 row in ``l10n_sk_kv_dph``). Returns made in the session are netted into it.

- An **invoiced POS order** is an invoice and stays in A.1 (or D.2 for a private customer); the receipt for its payment is not turnover.

- A journal with its own **Control-statement section override** keeps it.

Sections already stored on posted entries are not recomputed by installing the module; an administrator recomputes them, as the shared guide describes.

Limitations
=============

- Only the session's closing entry is recognised. A cash-register sale booked any other way still needs its journal set to ``D.1``.
