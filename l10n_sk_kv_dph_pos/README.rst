========================================
Slovakia — KV DPH D.1 from Point of Sale
========================================

Reports the closing entry of every Odoo Point of Sale session in oddiel
**D.1** of the Slovak kontrolný výkaz DPH (eKasa turnover) instead of D.2.
Installs itself when ``l10n_sk_kv_dph`` and ``point_of_sale`` are both
installed. See ``doc/en.md``.
