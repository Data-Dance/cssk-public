========================
CZ VAT Return (DPHDP3)
========================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The Czech VAT return computed from the tax tags of Odoo's Czech chart and exported as the EPO DPHDP3 XML, validated against the current official XSD for every period.**

What it is for
================

The Czech layer of the shared VAT-return engine. It supplies:

- the **DPHDP3** form (*přiznání k dani z přidané hodnoty*): its rows mapped to the tax tags of Odoo's Czech chart (``l10n_cz``), the EPO XML template (``Pisemnost / DPHDP3``) and the official schema;

- the filer block of the XML from the company's EPO data;

- bad-debt corrections reported on ř. 33 and ř. 34;

- repairs to the Czech chart's taxes without which several rows would stay empty or wrong;

- the archived rates in force before today's, for importing older documents.

How to create, compute, review, export and file a return is described on the engine's page; this page covers what is Czech.

Legal background
==================

- **Who files.** Every VAT payer (*plátce*) and identified person files a return — `§ 101 odst. 1 of the VAT Act <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__ — and a payer files one even for a period in which no tax arose (`§ 101 odst. 3 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__).

- **Tax period.** The calendar month (`§ 99 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__). A payer may choose the calendar quarter for a year if its domestic turnover for the preceding year did not exceed CZK 15 000 000, it is not an unreliable payer or a VAT group, and it notifies the tax office by the end of January (`§ 99a odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__).

- **Deadline.** For a tax period shorter than a year the return is filed within **25 days after the end of the period**, and the deadline cannot be extended — `§ 136 odst. 4 of the Tax Procedure Code <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280>`__.

- **Electronic filing in the prescribed structure.** A payer must file the return and any supplementary return electronically (`§ 101a odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__); a filing not made in the prescribed format or structure is ineffective (`§ 101a odst. 4 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__).

- **Corrective and supplementary returns.** Before the deadline a return may be replaced by an *opravné daňové přiznání* (`§ 138 of the Tax Procedure Code <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280>`__). After it, a payer that finds the tax should be higher than the last known tax files a *dodatečné daňové přiznání* by the end of the month following the month of discovery (`§ 141 odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280>`__), stating the difference against the last known tax and the day it was found (`§ 141 odst. 5 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280>`__).

- **Bad debts.** The creditor's correction of the tax base for an unrecoverable receivable is governed by `§ 46 and following of the VAT Act <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__, the debtor's matching reduction of its deduction by `§ 74b <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__.

The form and its structure
============================

The Czech Financial Administration publishes **one** DPHDP3 schema, ``dphdp3_epo2.xsd``, and replaces it in place; every return — for the current period or an old one — is filed in the structure current at the time of filing. The module therefore ships a single, open-ended version, **DPHDP3 (aktuální struktura)**, with that schema and the XML declared as ``verzePis="03.01.04"`` — structure 03.01.04 of 11. 8. 2026. Because nothing in the schema says which revision it is, the module pins the file's checksum and the structure version in ``data/SCHEMA_VERSION``, with the procedure for refreshing them; a test fails if the bundled file or the template's ``verzePis`` drifts from the pin. It is offered for periods from **1 January 2016**; that start date is a conservative bound chosen by the module, not a statutory one, and there is no version for earlier periods.

Amounts are exported in whole crowns: output tax and rows 61, 62 and 64–66 rounded **up** to the next crown, as § 146 odst. 1 of the daňový řád says ("Daň se zaokrouhluje na celé koruny nahoru"); a deduction (rows 40–47, the amounts of rows 52 and 53, row 60 and the total on row 63) rounded **down**, so that rounding never adds to it; a base or a value to the nearest crown, a half up; the coefficients of rows 52 and 53 up to a whole percent (§ 76 odst. 3 ZDPH). The filing date in the XML (*d_poddp*) is the day of the export. A return for a period in which every row is zero is marked as one in which no tax liability arose (*trans* = N in the XML); any figure makes it A.

Setting it up
===============

Beyond the engine's setup, the XML needs, on the company:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Where
     - What
   * - *Tax ID*
     - The DIČ (written without ``CZ``).
   * - *Finanční úřad*
     - The tax office; its submission code goes into *c_ufo*.
   * - *Finanční Úřad (CZ)*
     - The territorial office (*územní pracoviště*), *c_pracufo*.
   * - *Typ osoby / subjektu*
     - Decides whether the filer is a natural person or a legal entity.
   * - tab **EPO podání**
     - For a legal entity the *oprávněná osoba* (name and position), for a natural person the name and title; *Sestavil* (who prepared the return) and, if any, *Zástupce*.
   * - *NACE (main activity)*
     - The main CZ-NACE activity code, *c_okec*; EPO reports a return without it as a serious error.
   * - address, phone, e-mail
     - The filer's address (street and number are split), *c_telef*, *email*.

The payer type (*typ_platce*) is always **P** (payer) unless ``l10n_cz_vat_status`` records another status.

The Czech return
==================

The statement types are **Řádné** (B), **Opravné** (O), **Dodatečné** (D) and **Dodatečné/opravné** (E). Each row's code is the attribute name in the XML (``obrat23``, ``dan23``, ``odp_tuz23_nar`` …) and its name is the form's own caption, in Czech, as annex 1 of vyhláška č. 387/2025 Sb. states it, most specific part first (column — rate — row), e.g. *Základ daně — základní sazba — Dodání zboží nebo poskytnutí služby s místem plnění v tuzemsku*.

**Computed from tags**: rows 1–13 (base and tax), 20–26, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, the base and the full deduction of rows 40–44 and 47, row 50, 51 and 61.

**Computed from the other rows**, as the EPO structure defines them, after the rows above (each summed in whole crowns, as exported, so the totals foot):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Row
     - Formula
   * - 46
     - sum of rows 40–45, in each column (*v plné výši*, *krácený odpočet*)
   * - 62
     - rows 1–13 (tax) − row 61
   * - 63
     - row 46 *v plné výši* + row 52 + row 53 + row 60
   * - 64
     - row 62 − row 63 when positive (vlastní daň)
   * - 65
     - row 63 − row 62 when positive (nadměrný odpočet)
   * - 66
     - (row 62 − row 63) minus the same of the return amended, on a dodatečné return only

An **Override** on any of them is kept and feeds the rows after it. Rows 64 and 65 are left out of the XML of a dodatečné return, and row 66 out of any other. The tax under § 108 odst. 4 písm. g) and h), which the form adds to row 62 but has no row of its own, is not modelled: add it to row 62 with **Override** where it arises.

Rows 64 and 65 also drive the return's *Settlement* tab (amount to pay or refund, and the carry of an excess deduction).

**Manual** — enter them with **Override**, or they are exported as 0:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Row
     - Content
   * - 40–44, 47 (*reduced_claim*)
     - the deduction in reduced amount (*v krácené výši*)
   * - 45
     - correction of deduction
   * - 52, 53
     - coefficients and the settlement of the deduction
   * - 60
     - adjustment of deduction

**Content checks at export**: rows 64 and 65 must not be negative and must not both be filled.

**A dodatečné return** (types D and E) is created with **Amended / corrective** on the filed return, and states only the **differences** from it: "V dodatečném přiznání se uvádí pouze rozdíly od údajů, ze kterých byla stanovena poslední známá daň" (pokyny k přiznání). The rows on screen keep the period's full figures — they are what the next amendment differs from — and the XML files each row minus the same row of the return amended; the coefficients of rows 52 and 53 are filed as they are. A dodatečné/opravné return (E) raised from a dodatečné one replaces it, so it differs from what that dodatečné amended. Without the return it amends, the export refuses.

It also cannot be exported without **Důvody zjištěny dne** (*d_zjist*), shown next to the statement type. It is the same date as the engine's *Den zjištění rozdílu* (one field, shown once under its Czech name), so an amendment created with the button already carries it.

**Filing through EPO.** With ``l10n_cz_epo`` installed, **File it** offers the EPO channel: the return can be checked in EPO's test mode and filed with the company's certificate.

Repairs to the Czech chart
============================

On install and whenever a company loads the Czech chart, the module adjusts the chart's taxes so that the return can report them:

- **Deduction of self-assessed tax (ř. 43 and 44).** ``l10n_cz`` tags only the declaration of a reverse charge or an EU acquisition, so the deduction reached no row. The deduction legs get new tags *VAT 43 Base/Total* and *VAT 44 Base/Total*, the band taken from the row the tax declares on.

- **Import of goods (ř. 7 and 8).** Those taxes had a single tax leg, so a bill from a non-EU supplier asked you to pay the supplier the Czech VAT on top of the invoice; a cancelling deduction leg is added. Row 9 (new means of transport) is left as it is.

- **Credit notes on EU reverse charges.** Four taxes named the tax account on the invoice side only; the refund side gets the same account, so a reverse-charge credit note no longer leaves its VAT on the expense account.

- **Intra-Community purchases.** Under the *Intra-Community* fiscal position the domestic purchase taxes (21 % and 12 %, goods and services) are mapped to the EU acquisition taxes, so a bill from an EU supplier self-assesses the tax instead of keeping the Czech rate. Only links are added; a mapping set by hand is kept.

- **Intra-Community sales of services.** ``l10n_cz`` also names the domestic goods sale taxes as the originals of ``0% EU S``, so a goods line got both ``0% EU G`` and ``0% EU S`` and its base was declared twice (ř. 20 and 21, in the kontrolní hlášení and in the souhrnné hlášení), while a service with ``21% S`` kept Czech VAT. ``0% EU S`` now replaces ``21% S`` and ``12% S`` only. Posted documents keep their taxes.

- **Extra-Community purchases.** The same fault on the *Extra-Community* position mapped one goods line onto three taxes and declared it on ř. 7, 12 and 42, and left a service bought outside the EU at Czech 21 %. Goods and services are now mapped apart.

- **Asset acquisitions (ř. 47).** ``l10n_cz`` ships no tax for *hodnota pořízeného majetku*, so ř. 47 was always 0.00. Every purchase tax deductible on ř. 40–44 gets a *Majetek* twin (``21% G Majetek``, ``21% EU G Majetek``, …), identical except that it also reports the base on ř. 47. Pick the twin on the line of a bill for a long-term asset.

Bad-debt corrections
======================

A document flagged with **Oprava u nedobytné pohledávky** (a field added by ``l10n_cz_statutory``) is reported twice, as the Financial Administration's information of 4 December 2019 requires. It stays on its ordinary row, ř. 1/2 for the creditor and ř. 40/41 for the debtor, where it lowers the tax due. Its tax is also shown on **ř. 33** when it is a sales document or **ř. 34** when it is a purchase document, in addition to any entry tagged *VAT 33* / *VAT 34* by hand. ř. 62 and ř. 63 do not add ř. 33 and ř. 34.

Overdue bills: the deduction under § 74b
==========================================

From 1 January 2025 a VAT payer who has not paid a supplier's bill in full by the **last day of the sixth calendar month after the month in which it fell due** must reduce the deduction claimed, in proportion to the unpaid part, in the tax period that day belongs to: `§ 74b odst. 3 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__. When the bill is paid later, the deduction comes back in proportion to what was paid (odst. 4). The rule does not apply to a deduction that arose before 2025 (Act No. 461/2024 Sb., čl. II bod 17).

**Preparing the corrections.** *Accounting → Accounting → Oprava odpočtu daně (§ 74b)*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - Meaning
   * - Ke dni
     - Bills whose six months ran out by this day are offered.
   * - Zdaňovací období
     - Month or quarter. A quarterly payer owes no correction for a bill paid in full by the end of the quarter in which the six months ran out; a bill paid in part by then is corrected for what was still unpaid at the end of the quarter.
   * - Datum opravy
     - *Poslední den lhůty* dates each correction on the day the law names. If that period is locked the wizard refuses and names the bills: reopen the period and file a dodatečné přiznání, or choose *Den uvedený výše* to report the corrections on the day entered.
   * - Účet nákladu
     - Where the tax that is no longer deductible is booked; 538 is proposed.

The list shows the bills found: posted vendor bills of the company with a deduction, a taxable supply date from 1 January 2025, the time limit run out, not fully paid on the deciding day and not corrected yet. Payments made after the deciding day do not remove a bill from the list; they give the deduction back afterwards.

**Připravit opravy** creates one draft document per bill and opens them. Check and post them. Each is a vendor credit note for **zero**:

- a line with the bill's own tax, for the unpaid share of the base, which lowers ř. 40 / 41 by base and tax;

- a line taking the base back and a line booking the tax to the cost account, so the supplier's balance does not change (the debt remains).

It carries the supplier's number of the bill and the flag *Oprava u nedobytné pohledávky* = P, so its tax is also on **ř. 34** and it is a row of **B.2** in the kontrolní hlášení whatever its amount (`Bad-debt corrections <#bad-debt>`__). The entry is cost account debit / 343 credit.

**Giving the deduction back.** On the corrected bill, after a payment, the button **Vrátit odpočet (§ 74b odst. 4)** prepares the mirror document, a vendor bill for zero dated today, for the tax of what was paid since the correction and not yet given back. It can be used after each part payment. It is refused after the end of the second calendar year following the last payment (odst. 9).

The bill shows where it stands under *Other Info*: *Stav § 74b* and the documents made for it.

Each is cloned from today's rate of the same band, because DPHDP3 keeps the standard rate (rows 1/2, deduction row 40) apart from the reduced one (rows 3/4, row 41):

Limitations
=============

- **§ 74b covers the overdue bill (odst. 3 and 4) only**, not the corrections that follow a creditor's correction of an unrecoverable receivable (odst. 1 and 2); flag those documents by hand. Bills under reverse charge are left out: no VAT is owed to the supplier there. Bills payable in instalments are listed in the wizard but not corrected, because each instalment has its own time limit. "Paid" means reconciled in Odoo, dated by the later of the two entries reconciled; a supplier's credit note set against the bill counts as payment. Whether the bill is unpaid is read from the books, so reconcile payments before running the wizard.

- The reduced-amount deductions, rows 45, 52, 53 and 60, are manual (see `above <#usage>`__), and so is the § 108 odst. 4 g)/h) part of row 62.

- The content checks cover rows 64 and 65 only; the schema cannot tell whether the other figures are consistent.

- Periods before 2016 have no version.

- A dodatečné return differs from the return it was created from. Create it from the last return filed for the period, so that return is the last known tax.

- The pin in ``data/SCHEMA_VERSION`` shows that the bundled schema is the one verified on a stated date, not that it is still current; refresh it by the procedure in that file.

- The rows follow ``l10n_cz``'s tags. A tax created by hand without the right tags reaches the wrong row or none.
