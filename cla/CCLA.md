# Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement

**Data Dance s.r.o.** — v1.0

This agreement is for a **legal entity** whose employees or contractors
contribute to software published by Data Dance s.r.o. ("**Data Dance**").

If you are contributing as an individual and no employer holds rights in your
work, sign the [Individual CLA](ICLA.md) instead. If your employer holds rights
in what you produce — which is the default position under most employment
relationships — your employer must sign this agreement.

## 1. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the legal entity identified in the schedule below and
all other entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control
with that entity. For this purpose, *control* means direct or indirect control
of 50 % or more of the shares or voting rights, or the power to direct the
management of the entity.

**"Contribution"** and **"Submitted"** have the meanings given in the
[Individual CLA](ICLA.md), sections 1.

**"Authorised Contributor"** means an employee or contractor of the Corporation
listed in Schedule A, as amended from time to time in accordance with section 6.

## 2. Grant of copyright licence

Subject to the terms of this agreement, the Corporation grants to Data Dance and
to recipients of software distributed by Data Dance a **perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence** to reproduce,
prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and
distribute the Contributions of its Authorised Contributors and such derivative
works.

**The Corporation expressly agrees that Data Dance may license and sublicense
those Contributions, and any derivative works of them, under licence terms of
Data Dance's choosing** — including terms that differ from those under which the
Contribution was submitted, and including both open-source and proprietary or
commercial licences, with or without payment of a fee to Data Dance. The
Corporation will not be entitled to any compensation, royalty or accounting in
respect of such licensing.

## 3. Grant of patent licence

The Corporation grants a patent licence on the same terms as section 3 of the
[Individual CLA](ICLA.md), applying to patent claims licensable by the
Corporation that are necessarily infringed by a Contribution of an Authorised
Contributor, alone or in combination with the Work.

## 4. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

a. It is legally entitled to grant the above licences, and that each Authorised
   Contributor is authorised to submit Contributions on its behalf.

b. Each Contribution submitted by an Authorised Contributor is either the
   original creation of that person, or the Corporation holds sufficient rights
   in it to grant the licences above.

c. Contributions do not, to the best of the Corporation's knowledge, infringe or
   misappropriate the rights of any third party.

d. Where a Contribution was produced with the assistance of an automated or
   generative tool, the Corporation has verified that it holds or has been
   granted all rights necessary to submit it under this agreement.

## 5. Third-party works

Contributions that are not the original creation of an Authorised Contributor
must be submitted separately, identifying the complete details of their source
and of any licence or other restriction of which the Corporation is aware, and
conspicuously marked
"*Submitted on behalf of a third party: [name]*".

## 6. Maintaining Schedule A

The Corporation shall keep Schedule A current and shall notify Data Dance
promptly when a person is added to or removed from it. A person removed from
Schedule A remains bound by the licences granted in respect of Contributions
made while listed.

Data Dance may accept a Contribution from a person not listed in Schedule A only
where that person has signed the Individual CLA in their own right.

## 7. No obligation, no warranty

Sections 7 (No obligation), 8 (No warranty) and 9 (Notification) of the
[Individual CLA](ICLA.md) apply equally to the Corporation.

## 8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of the Slovak Republic, excluding its
conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this
agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Slovak
Republic competent for the registered seat of Data Dance s.r.o.
