[19.0.1.0.0] — 2026-11-12
-------------------------

Added
~~~~~

- First release in l10n_cssk, replacing ``l10n_cities_sk`` (15.0/16.0).
  The data is regenerated by ``tools/gen_base_location_data.py`` from the
  post's current list; ``data/SOURCE`` records the inputs and the counts.

Fixed (against ``l10n_cities_sk``)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- **Obce with the same name are separate cities.** The old data keyed an obec
  by its name alone, so the about 100 Slovak names shared by several obce
  (Lúčka ×4, Porúbka ×4, Riečka, Lieskovec …) became one city each, carrying
  the PSČ of all of its namesakes and the okres of whichever came first. An
  obec is now keyed by okres and name.
- **Full names.** City names come from the post's full-name column
  ("Bohdanovce nad Trnavou", not "Bohdanovce n/Trnavou"), and the okres
  "Nové Mesto nad Váhom" is no longer abbreviated.
- **Bratislava and Košice are one city each** with every PSČ of the town,
  taken from the post's street list. The old data listed their mestské časti
  from a hand-made sheet that left nine parts of Bratislava with no PSČ and
  had wrong codes in others (841 04 written 941 04). A part of Bratislava or
  Košice spans no single okres for the post, so the two towns carry none.
