================================================
Slovak Districts, Municipalities and Postcodes
================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Every Slovak okres, obec and PSČ, so that typing a postcode or a town on a contact fills in the town, the postcode, the district and the country.**

What it is for
================

The OCA module *Location management* (``base_location``) adds a **ZIP Location** field to contacts and companies: type a postcode or the beginning of a town and pick from the list, and the address is filled in for you. On its own it ships no Slovak data. This module adds it:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - In Odoo
     - In Slovakia
     - How many
   * - State
     - okres, with its kraj ("okres Lučenec, Banskobystrický kraj")
     - 79
   * - City
     - obec
     - about 2,900
   * - ZIP
     - PSČ, written as on a Slovak document ("985 13")
     - about 3,200

Obce sharing a name are separate entries, each in its own okres (there are four called Lúčka), so pick by the okres shown next to the name.

**Bratislava and Košice** are one city each, with every postcode of the town and no okres: a town part spans no single okres for the post office, and an address there is written "851 01 Bratislava". Picking one of their postcodes clears an okres left over from an earlier address.

Installing
============

It is not installed with the Slovak localization package. Install it from *Apps* when you want the postcode picker; it brings ``base_location`` with it.

Where the data comes from
===========================

From the lists Slovenská pošta publishes at posta.sk (obce, the post-office detail for obce served by several offices, and the street list for towns with more than one postcode). The file ``data/SOURCE`` in the module says which download it was built from. A newer release of the module adds new obce and postcodes on update; it never removes or renames an entry you may already use on a contact, and edits you make to the entries are kept.
