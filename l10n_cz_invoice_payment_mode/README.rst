==========================
CZ Invoice: Forma úhrady
==========================

Prints the invoice's payment mode (OCA ``account_payment_mode``) as *Forma
úhrady* beside the payment symbols in the header of the Czech invoice.
Installs automatically where both ``l10n_cz_invoice`` and
``account_payment_mode`` are installed.

To pay cash and card invoices on validation, see
``account_payment_mode_pay_on_post``.
