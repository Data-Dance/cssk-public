===================================================
SK Monthly Health Insurance Statement (dávka 514)
===================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The monthly výkaz preddavkov na poistné for a Slovak health insurer (VšZP, Dôvera, Union) in the common dávka 514 format, built from the month's payslips and exported as XML.**

What it is for
================

Every month the employer reports to each **health insurer** the health insurance advances (*preddavky na poistné*) it pays for its employees and for itself: the *mesačný výkaz preddavkov na poistné*. All three Slovak insurers — **VšZP** (25), **Dôvera** (24) and **Union** (27) — accept it in the same electronic format, **dávka 514**.

This module builds the dávka 514 from the payslips of the month and exports it as an XML file to upload through the insurer's electronic branch. Payslips are read through ``l10n_cssk_payroll_declaration_base``, so it works with either Slovak payroll engine — see the Slovak payroll guide.

Legal background
==================

References are to the `Health Insurance Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/>`__ (*zákon o zdravotnom poistení*).

- **The statement.** The employer reports to the competent health insurer the advances for the calendar month, broken down by the employees for whom it pays them and for the employer itself (the *mesačný výkaz*). It states the total income, the income for and the amount of the deductible item, tax- exempt payments, the assessment base and the advance, and must be sent **electronically** — `§ 20 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-20.odsek-1>`__.

- **The due date of the advances.** The employee's and employer's advances are due on the day fixed for paying out the employees' income for the month; if no such day is fixed, on the last day of the following month — `§ 17 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-17.odsek-1>`__.

- **Corrections.** Data the insurer rejects in its error report (*chybový protokol*) must be corrected electronically within 30 days of receiving it — `§ 24 ods. 1 písm. m) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-24.odsek-1.pismeno-m>`__.

Setting it up
===============

On the company:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Value
     - Where
     - Used as
   * - *Company ID* (IČO), 8 to 12 digits
     - company form
     - ``IDCode``, ``CompanyIDCode``
   * - *DIČ*, 10 digits
     - company form (from the Slovak base module)
     - ``CompanyIDTaxCode``
   * - *Health payer number*
     - company form, or the dávka 514 form
     - ``CodeOfPayer`` — the number the insurer assigned to the employer
   * - *Health insurer (dávka 514)*
     - company form
     - the default insurer of a new statement (VšZP if not set)
   * - a bank account
     - the company's contact
     - ``IBAN`` — the first account is used

Both health fields sit on the company form, tab *Payroll Reporting*, group *Health insurance (dávka 514)*. The *Health payer number* can also be filled in on the dávka 514 form itself; it is stored on the company either way, so it is entered once (changing it needs the right to edit companies).

Every employee on the statement needs the **birth number** in *Identification No*, and should carry their **Health insurer (SK)**, next to it on the *Personal* tab. Each dávka 514 takes only the employees insured with the insurer it is addressed to; an employee whose insurer is left empty counts as insured with the company's default insurer (VšZP unless changed). Set the insurer on every employee rather than relying on the default.

Preparing the statement
=========================

*Payroll Declarations → SK dávka 514 (health)*, then *New*.

1. Choose the **Health insurer**, the **Year** and the **Month**. *Payment Date* gives the day of payment reported in ``DateOfPayment``; left empty, the last day of the month is used.

2. Click **Generate**. The module takes the company's confirmed payslips whose period ends in the month, builds one row per employee and renders the XML.

3. Upload the file through the insurer's electronic branch and click **Mark Submitted**. **Reset to Draft** reopens it.

Generation stops if the IČO, the DIČ, the payer number or the insurer is missing, if an employee has no valid birth number, or if there are no payslips for the month.

**Who is left out.** A DoVP or DoPČ worker is not reported, and does not count as an employee of the insurer, when the contract the payslip was computed on has either - a **Pension granted (SK)**: the statute excludes agreement income of every pensioner group the field offers, with no income limit or claim (`§ 10b ods. 1 písm. a) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-10b.odsek-1.pismeno-a>`__); or - **Health-insurance exempt** ticked without a pension, for example a worker covered by another state's legislation (an A1 form). Keeping that evidence is up to you.

An ordinary employment contract stays on the statement even with a pension. A DoVP or DoPČ with a pension but *Health-insurance exempt* not ticked stops generation: its payslip carries health advances for income the statute excludes. Tick the box on that contract and recalculate the payslip. The check covers only the insurer the statement is addressed to.

What the statement contains
=============================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Part
     - Content
   * - ``Identification``
     - type 514, character *N*, IČO, payer number, the insurer's four-digit code (2500, 2400, 2700), date of sending, serial number 1, number of rows
   * - ``CorporateBody``
     - period, day of payment, company name, IČO, payer number, DIČ, IBAN
   * - ``InsuranceBody``
     - employer totals: number of employees, days, income, assessment base, employer and employee advances, top-up, total
   * - ``PersonData``
     - per employee: birth number, days, the employer's and employee's rate, income, assessment base, advances, top-up

The amounts come from the payslip lines: *HEALTHEMPLOYER* (employer's advance), *HEALTH* (employee's advance) and the health minimum-base top-up (*HEALTHDOPLATOK* or *HEALTH_DOPLATOK*, depending on the engine). The assessment base and the income are the employee's *GROSS*. The rates are not read from a table: they are worked back as advance ÷ base.

**Checking the file.** The official schema ``514-2023.xsd`` contradicts itself (patterns that admit only one digit in fields that must be 8 to 12 characters long, impossible numeric limits), so no real file can pass it. The module therefore runs it without stopping and checks the structure instead: the root element, the three header blocks and that the number of rows matches.

Limitations
=============

- **The insurer is a property of the employee, not of the month.** A change of insurer during the year (allowed once a year, from 1 January) has to be recorded on the employee before generating; past statements are not recomputed.

- **The assessment base is the payslip's GROSS**, not a base computed under the Health Insurance Act; the rate shown is derived from it.

- Every employee counts as a full calendar month of days, and everyone is reported in the first group (the second group of counts and bases is always zero).

- Only a regular statement (*N*) with serial number 1. Corrections after an error report must be made through the insurer's electronic branch.

- Because the official schema cannot be applied, the module cannot guarantee that the insurer will accept the file. Reconcile the first statement with the insurer.
