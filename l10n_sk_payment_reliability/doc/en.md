Answers the supplier reliability check for Slovak suppliers from the Finančná správa open-data service: the bank accounts the supplier published (by IČ DPH), its index daňovej spoľahlivosti (by IČO) and whether it is on the list of payers with grounds for cancelling the registration.

Install it together with the reliability check and the Slovak chart of accounts (`l10n_sk`). It needs a free API key from `opendata.financnasprava.sk`, entered in the **FS open-data API key** field of the *Supplier reliability check (CZ/SK)* setting; without the key Slovak suppliers are not checked.
