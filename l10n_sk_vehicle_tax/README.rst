Slovakia — motor vehicle tax
============================

Prepare calendar-year DMVv25 returns for Slovak legal entities using reviewed
L/M1/N1 vehicle facts, dated rates and the shared statutory export pipeline.

Technical data comes from native fleet.vehicle records, including vehicles
sold or archived during the year. Reviewed filing snapshots preserve the
source facts. No mileage log is required. Actual event dates are normalized
to statutory months; duplicate regular filings are refused even across workers.

Supports ordinary, corrective and additional returns, exact source/XML
snapshots, age-rate transitions, electric vehicles, documented ecological
reductions, paid advances, projected-tax proposals and bank overpayment
requests. Calculation does not create a second accounting ledger or initiate
payments, signatures, transport or authority acceptance.

See ``doc/en.md`` and ``doc/sk.md`` for scope, review inputs and legal sources.
