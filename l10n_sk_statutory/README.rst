=============================
SK Statutory Reference Data
=============================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The Slovak tax offices and taxpayer types, so that a company can name the tax office its electronic filings go to.**

What it is for
================

Slovak electronic filings to the Financial Administration — the VAT return (*daňové priznanie k DPH*) and the EC sales list (*súhrnný výkaz*) among them — name the tax office (*daňový úrad*) they are addressed to. This module fills the registers of ``l10n_cssk_core`` with Slovak data:

- **the Slovak tax offices**: the eight regional offices *Daňový úrad Bratislava, Trnava, Trenčín, Nitra, Žilina, Banská Bystrica, Prešov* and *Košice*, and the *Daňový úrad pre vybrané daňové subjekty* (the office for selected taxpayers), each with its address and switchboard number;

- **the taxpayer types** *Natural person* and *Legal entity* (*Fyzická osoba* and *Právnická osoba* in Slovak and Czech).

Keeping this data in a Slovak module means none of it loads on a Czech database.

Setting it up
===============

*Settings → Users & Companies → Companies*, open the company and set:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - What to enter
   * - Tax Authority (*Daňový úrad*)
     - The tax office the company is registered with.
   * - Entity Type (*Typ subjektu*)
     - *Legal entity* or *Natural person*.

The tax office can also be set under *Accounting → Configuration → Settings*, block *Czech / Slovak Localization*, field *Default tax authority*. The list of offices is under *Accounting → Configuration → CZ/SK Localization → Tax Authorities*.

How the filings use it
========================

The Slovak filing schemas have no code list for tax offices: the office is a free-text element (``danovyUrad``). The *Submission Code* (*Kód podania*) of each office therefore holds its full name, for example *Daňový úrad Žilina*, and that text is what ``l10n_sk_vat_return`` and ``l10n_sk_ec_sales`` write into the file. Leave it unchanged unless the Financial Administration expects a different wording.

Limitations
=============

- The list of offices is curated by hand; there is no official list in Odoo to load it from. If offices are merged or renamed, edit the records.

- Addresses and telephone numbers are not maintained automatically; check them if you use them.

- No Slovak filing reads the *Entity Type* at present; it is recorded for completeness.

- The records are loaded once and are not overwritten on upgrade, so your edits are kept, but corrections made in a later version do not reach an existing database either.
