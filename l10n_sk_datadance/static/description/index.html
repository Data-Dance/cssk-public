Slovak Accounting Localization (Data Dance) — full suite

One-click meta-module for the Slovak statutory core, with cross-form reconciliation

Installing this app pulls in the Slovak statutory core in a single step: KV DPH (control statement), Súhrnný výkaz (EC sales list), DPH priznanie (VAT return), Súvaha + VZS (financial statements), DPPO (income tax), SK invoice, VIES, saldokonto + zápočet and dohadné (accruals).

Optional workflow pieces (FX sync, deferrals, the supplier-reliability check, dual depreciation, Method A inventory and advance invoices) are selectable in Settings → Accounting → Slovak localization — tick the box to install. On top of bundling, the module adds cross-form reconciliation between the filed statements and the ledger.

Key features

  • Meta-installer for the Slovak statutory core (one dependency set).
  • Settings toggles for the optional workflow modules.
  • Cross-form reconciliation (porovnanie) between KV DPH, súhrnný výkaz, DPH priznanie, VZS/DPPO and the ledger account 343.

Usage

Install the module to provision the Slovak core. Open Settings ▸ Accounting ▸ Slovak localization (Data Dance) to tick the optional pieces you need. The reconciliation checks are launched from the statement forms (see Wizards).

Wizards

SK cross-form reconciliation (buttons on the statement forms — l10n.sk.dph.reconciliation)

What it does: an advisory comparison (porovnanie) between the Slovak statutory statements that share the same tax-tagged move lines. KV ⊆ DP relationships are reported as expected non-KV content; only structurally impossible cases are flagged.

How to use: open a statement (out of draft) and press the matching button:

  • On the DPH priznanie form — Porovnať s účtovníctvom: ties output VAT (r17) and input deduction to account 343, and reports the base-vs-revenue (trieda 60) gap as informational.
  • On the Súhrnný výkaz form — Porovnať s priznaním: ties EC-sales goods (code 0) to priznanie r14.
  • On the DPPO form — Porovnať s účtovnou závierkou: ties the accounting result before tax (VZS r56) to DPPO r100 (an exact tie).
