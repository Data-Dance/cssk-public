================================
ISDOC — Czech Advance Invoices
================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Exports the tax document for a received advance payment as ISDOC type 5 (6 for its credit note) and reports advances deducted on the final invoice as deposits.**

Maps the advance chain of ``sale_order_advance_invoice`` to ISDOC: a tax document for a received payment (*daňový doklad k přijaté platbě*) is exported as document type **5**, its credit note as **6**, and on the final invoice each advance deduction is reported as a taxed or untaxed deposit, with the number and variable symbol of the advance's tax document and the VAT already claimed, instead of as an ordinary line.

It installs itself when ``account_edi_isdoc_sale`` and ``sale_order_advance_invoice`` are both installed, and has no settings.
