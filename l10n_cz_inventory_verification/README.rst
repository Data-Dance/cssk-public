===========================================
CZ Inventory Verification (inventarizace)
===========================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The Czech inventory verification as records — the inventarizace, its inventurní soupisy with every particular § 30 odst. 7 of the Accounting Act prescribes, and an internal closing record comparing them with the books.**

What it is for
================

A Czech accounting entity must establish the actual state of all its assets and liabilities and verify that it matches the books. This is the **inventarizace** (inventory verification). Each count or check within it is an **inventura**, and what an inventura finds is written down in an **inventurní soupis** (count sheet), a record whose content the Accounting Act prescribes.

The module keeps those records in Odoo: one inventarizace document per exercise, any number of inventurní soupisy under it, and a closing record — the **inventarizační zápis** — on the inventarizace itself. For an account verified from documents, the book balance is read from the general ledger. Both records print as PDF.

It records and evidences the exercise. It does **not** post inventory differences: manka, schodky, přebytky, opravné položky and the like follow from it and are entered by the accountant.

The statutory field names and the printouts are in Czech, because they are the statute's own terms. This page quotes them as they appear.

Legal background
==================

All references are to the `Accounting Act <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1991/563>`__ unless stated otherwise.

- **When.** Under `§ 29 odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1991/563#p29>`__ the inventarizace is carried out to the moment the ordinary or extraordinary financial statements are drawn up (**periodická inventarizace**). Under § 29 odst. 2 a **průběžná inventarizace** during the period is allowed only for stock accounted for by kind, by place of storage or by hmotně odpovědná osoba, and for movable tangible fixed assets that are constantly on the move; each kind must be verified at least once in the period.

- **How.** Under `§ 30 odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1991/563#p30>`__ the actual state is established by a **fyzická inventura** for assets whose existence can be established visually, and by a **dokladová inventura** for liabilities and for assets whose existence cannot — including off-balance-sheet items. An inventarizace consists of one or more inventury (§ 30 odst. 2).

- **The inventurní soupis** is a průkazný účetní záznam that must contain, under § 30 odst. 7: the facts found, so that the assets and liabilities can be identified unambiguously (písm. a); the podpisový záznam of the *osoba odpovědná za zjištění skutečností* and of the *osoba odpovědná za provedení inventury* (písm. b); the *způsob zjišťování skutečných stavů* (písm. c); the valuation as at the rozvahový den for a periodická inventarizace (písm. d) or as at the day the inventura ended for a průběžná one (písm. e), in either case optionally also as at the rozhodný den; the *okamžik, ke kterému se sestavuje účetní závěrka* (písm. f); the *rozhodný den* where the entity set one (písm. g); and the *okamžik zahájení a okamžik ukončení inventury* (písm. h).

- **Rozhodný den.** Under § 30 odst. 4 the entity may establish the actual state as at a day preceding the rozvahový den and complete it from the records of additions and disposals between the two days.

- **Time window.** Under § 30 odst. 6 an inventura of a periodická inventarizace may start four months before the rozvahový den at the earliest and must end two months after it at the latest.

- **Differences.** Under § 30 odst. 10 an *inventarizační rozdíl* is a difference between the actual state and the books that cannot be proven in the way the Act lays down: a *manko* (a *schodek* for cash and ceniny) where the actual state is lower, a *přebytek* where it is higher. They are booked in the period being verified (§ 30 odst. 11).

- **Valuation.** § 29 odst. 1 also has the entity verify whether there are reasons to account for the items of § 25 odst. 3 — foreseeable risks, possible losses and impairment. For businesses, `vyhláška č. 500/2002 Sb. <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2002/500>`__ adds that the amount and justification of opravné položky (§ 55 odst. 1) and of rezervy (§ 57 odst. 5) are assessed at the inventarizace.

- **Evidence.** The entity must be able to prove that the inventarizace was carried out for five years afterwards (§ 29 odst. 3).

**The inventarizační zápis is not a statutory document.** The Act prescribes the inventurní soupis and nothing that closes the exercise. Businesses commonly draw up an *inventarizační zápis* (or *zpráva*) as an internal record, and that is what this module prints: it is how the comparison of § 30 odst. 2 and the valuation assessment of § 29 odst. 1 are written down and kept. An *inventarizační zpráva* with prescribed content exists only for vybrané účetní jednotky (the public sector), under vyhláška č. 270/2010 Sb., which does not apply to businesses and which this module does not implement.

Setting it up
===============

Nothing to configure. The documents are numbered ``INV/<year>/001``, ``INV/<year>/002``, … from one sequence shared by all companies; the year is the year the document is created, not the year being verified.

The menu is *Accounting → Accounting → Inventarizace*. On Community the app is called *Invoicing*, and the *Accounting* submenu is shown only to users with access to the full accounting features. Invoicing users may create and edit inventarizace; only accounting managers may delete them.

Carrying out an inventarizace
===============================

1. **Create the inventarizace.** *Inventarizace → New*, choose the *Druh inventarizace* (**Periodická** or **Průběžná**) and fill *Okamžik, ke kterému se sestavuje účetní závěrka* — the rozvahový den. Click **Start** to move it from *Draft* to *In progress*.

2. **Add the inventurní soupisy** on the tab **Inventurní soupisy**, one per inventura — one per warehouse, cash desk, asset group or account. *Add a line* opens the soupis in its own window. Under **Inventura (§ 30 odst. 7)** fill:

3. *Name* and *Druh inventury*: **Fyzická inventura** or **Dokladová inventura**;

4. *Způsob zjišťování skutečných stavů* — in your own words, for example *počítáním a vážením* or *odsouhlasením zůstatků*;

5. *Okamžik zahájení inventury* and *Okamžik ukončení inventury*, with the time of day;

6. *Rozhodný den*, only if you set one; for a periodická inventarizace it must precede the rozvahový den;

7. *Osoba odpovědná za zjištění skutečností* and *Osoba odpovědná za provedení inventury* (users; the same person may hold both).

*Místo uložení majetku* and *Hmotně odpovědná osoba* are optional: the Act does not ask for them on the soupis. 3. **Enter the items** (*Items*) in the same window: *Description*, optionally *Product*, *Quantity*, *Unit* (free text) and *Unit price*. The *Ocenění* is quantity × unit price, and the soupis's *Skutečný stav* is the sum of its items. Choosing a product fills nothing in; type the price yourself. Describe each item so that it can be identified unambiguously — an inventory number, a serial number, a document number. 4. **Book state.** - *Dokladová inventura* with an *Account*: *Stav v účetnictví* is the account's balance from **posted** entries dated up to and including the day shown as *Ocenění ke dni* — the rozvahový den for a periodická inventarizace, the day the inventura ended for a průběžná one. Entries posted later are picked up by **Read account balance** in the soupis, or **Read book balances** at the top of the inventarizace for every dokladová inventura at once. - *Fyzická inventura*: type the *Stav v účetnictví* from your stock or asset records.

*Difference* is *Skutečný stav* − *Stav v účetnictví*, per soupis and in total. 5. **Write the closing record** on the tab **Inventarizační zápis**: *Výsledky porovnání skutečného stavu se stavem v účetnictví* (including the inventarizační rozdíly and how they are to be booked), *Posouzení ocenění majetku a závazků* and *Osoba odpovědná za provedení inventarizace*. 6. Click **Close**. It is refused when the inventarizace has no soupis, or when any soupis lacks one of the particulars a person has to supply under § 30 odst. 7: either of the two responsible persons, the method, or the moment the inventura started or ended. The message names the soupis and what is missing. **Back to draft** reopens it at any stage.

The time window
-----------------

When an inventura of a periodická inventarizace starts more than four months before the rozvahový den or ends more than two months after it, a warning is shown at the top of the inventarizace (§ 30 odst. 6). It is a warning only: the moments an inventura really took place can always be recorded.

Working with a rozhodný den
-----------------------------

The valuation on the soupis is always as at the rozvahový den. When you count as at an earlier rozhodný den, enter the items as they stood on the rozvahový den — the count adjusted by the additions and disposals in between — and keep the records of those movements with the soupis.

Signs on accounts verified from documents
-------------------------------------------

The book balance is debit minus credit, so liability and equity accounts come out **negative**: a supplier balance of 500 on account 321 reads −500. Enter the confirmed amounts of such an account with a negative unit price (for example, quantity 1, price −500) so that *Difference* compares like with like.

Printouts
===========

**Print inventurní soupisy** at the top of the inventarizace prints its soupisy, one per page: the entity's name, seat and IČO, the kind of inventarizace and inventura, the method, the rozvahový den, the rozhodný den (or *nestanoven*), the moments the inventura started and ended, the place and account where given, the items with quantity, unit, unit price and valuation, the totals with the day of valuation, notes, and signature lines for the two persons § 30 odst. 7 písm. b) names. A third line for the hmotně odpovědná osoba is printed only when one is filled in.

From an inventarizace, *Print → Inventarizační zápis* produces the closing record, headed as an internal record: the entity's name, seat and IČO, the rozvahový den, the span of the inventury, a table of every soupis with book state, actual state and difference and their totals, the comparison text, the valuation assessment (or *Neuvedeno*), and signature lines for the person responsible for the inventarizace and for the entity.

The seat is the company's address and the IČO its company registration number; the IČO line is left out when that is empty.

Signatures are blank lines to be signed on paper; nothing is signed electronically. If you keep the records electronically only, attach a podpisový záznam that meets § 33a of the Act yourself.

Limitations
=============

- The soupis carries one valuation, as at the rozvahový den (or the end of the inventura for a průběžná inventarizace). The optional second valuation as at the rozhodný den, which § 30 odst. 7 písm. d) and e) allow, has no field of its own; use the item notes.

- The day of valuation of a průběžná inventarizace is the day the inventura ended in the time zone of the user who saved it.

- § 30 odst. 8 lets a průběžná inventarizace replace the soupis by a record of the physical count and of the settlement of differences. The module always produces a soupis.

- Nothing checks that a průběžná inventarizace is confined to the assets § 29 odst. 2 allows, or that each kind is verified once a period.

- *Osoba odpovědná za provedení inventarizace* holds one user and is not required to close.

- For a dokladová inventura a typed-over book balance does not last: the next recalculation or refresh replaces it.

- On a fyzická inventura nothing is read from Odoo Inventory or the asset register; the book state is typed.

- A difference is shown as a number. Whether it is a manko, a schodek or a přebytek — or no inventarizační rozdíl at all, because it can be proven — is for the accountant to state in the closing record.

- A closed inventarizace stays editable — *Done* locks nothing.

- No differences are posted; manka, přebytky and valuation adjustments are entered by the accountant. For stock, the Czech accounting standard for businesses No. 007 deals with inventory differences and losses within the norms of natural loss.

- The rules of vyhláška č. 270/2010 Sb. for vybrané účetní jednotky (plán inventur, inventarizační komise, inventarizační zpráva) are not implemented.
