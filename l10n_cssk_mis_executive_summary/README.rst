=========================================
CZ/SK: Executive Summary (MIS Builder)
=========================================

|badge1|

.. |badge1| image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png

A MIS Builder template, *Executive Summary (CZ/SK)*, for the Czech and Slovak
charts of accounts (same account classes): cash at the start and end with
receipts and payments, revenue, cost of sales, gross and net profit and
margins, trade receivables and payables with debtor and creditor days, the
current ratio and return on assets. Add it to a MIS report instance with the
periods you want (month, quarter, year, compared with last year).

See the user guide for what each figure is made of.
