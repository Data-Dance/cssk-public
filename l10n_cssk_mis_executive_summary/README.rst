=========================
CZ/SK Executive Summary
=========================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**A one-page MIS Builder summary for Czech and Slovak charts of accounts - cash, revenue and margins, debtor and creditor days, current ratio and return on assets.**

What it is for
================

The figures a board asks for first, on one page, from the books: what came in and went out of cash, what was sold and at what margin, how long customers take to pay and how long we take, and whether the short-term debts are covered. It is a template for ``mis_builder``, built on the account classes the Czech and Slovak charts share.

Using it
==========

*Accounting › Reporting › MIS Reports*, *New*, template **Executive Summary (CZ/SK)**, then add the columns you want - this month, this year, the same period last year, a comparison. The figures follow the column's dates and filters; every figure drills down to its journal items.

What each figure is made of
=============================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Figure
     - Accounts
   * - Cash and bank at the start / end
     - 21x, 22x, 261 - opening and closing balance
   * - Cash received / spent
     - debit / credit movement on 21x, 22x, 261 (transfers between cash and bank count on both sides)
   * - Revenue
     - credit balance of 60x
   * - Cost of goods and material sold
     - 501, 504
   * - Gross profit
     - revenue − cost of goods and material sold
   * - Total income / Total expenses
     - class 6 / class 5
   * - Net profit
     - total income − total expenses
   * - Gross / net margin
     - gross / net profit ÷ revenue
   * - Trade receivables / payables
     - closing balance of 311 / 321
   * - Debtor days
     - receivables ÷ revenue × days in the period
   * - Creditor days
     - payables ÷ purchases (classes 50 and 51) × days in the period
   * - Current ratio
     - current assets (class 1, cash, debit balances of 31x, 33x–35x, 37x, 38x) ÷ current liabilities (credit balances of 23x, 24x, 32x–34x, 36x–38x)
   * - Return on assets
     - net profit ÷ total assets (classes 0 and 1, debit balances of classes 2 and 3)

Margins and ratios are not added up in a sum column; balances neither.

Limitations
=============

The figures are management indicators from account classes, not the statutory statements: a company that posts to unusual accounts may need the template copied and adjusted (*Duplicate* in MIS Builder). Return on assets uses the period's profit, so for a month it is a monthly figure.
