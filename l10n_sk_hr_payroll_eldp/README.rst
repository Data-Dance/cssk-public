==========================================
SK Pension Insurance Record Sheet (ELDP)
==========================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The evidenčný list dôchodkového poistenia for a calendar year — each employee's pension assessment base and insured period, built from the year's payslips and exported as a batch XML for the Sociálna poisťovňa.**

What it is for
================

**Periods before 1 January 2026 only.** The employer's ELDP filing obligation ended for periods from 2026. Data for those periods is provided through the applicable monthly statements. Historical filings and corrections for older periods remain available; new records default to 2025.

The *Evidenčný list dôchodkového poistenia* (**ELDP**) records, per employee and calendar year, the period of pension insurance and the assessment base on which pension premiums were paid. The Sociálna poisťovňa uses it to establish pension rights.

This module builds the electronic batch ELDP (one ``eldpZec`` record per employee) for a whole year from that year's payslips and exports it as XML. Payslips are read through ``l10n_cssk_payroll_declaration_base``, so it works with either Slovak payroll engine — see the Slovak payroll guide.

Legal background
==================

References are to the `Social Insurance Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/>`__.

- **The employer's records.** The employer keeps a record of each employee for social-insurance purposes for as long as the insurance lasts (`§ 232 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-232.odsek-1>`__); among other things it contains the start and end of the employment and the employee's **unlimited assessment base** (*neobmedzený vymeriavací základ*, `§ 232 ods. 2 písm. e) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-232.odsek-2.pismeno-e>`__).

- **The filing is abolished from 2026.** Act No. 310/2024 Z. z. deleted the employer's duty to submit this record (§ 231 ods. 1 písm. j) as in force until 31 December 2025) with effect from 1 January 2026. The Sociálna poisťovňa now takes the data from its own systems; the one item only the ELDP carried, the wage compensation for serious operational reasons (§ 142 ods. 4 and § 250b ods. 6 of the Labour Code), moves to the monthly report (`Sociálna poisťovňa announcement <https://www.socpoist.sk/news/povinnost-zamestnavatelov-predkladat-evidencne-listy-dochodkoveho-poistenia-sa-od-roku-2026>`__).

- **The one-off filing for periods before 2026.** `§ 293gl <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/461/#paragraf-293gl>`__ still requires the record for the period before 1 January 2026, for every employee, by:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Employees concerned
     - Deadline
   * - left before 2026, record never filed (or returned for correction)
     - 31 January 2026
   * - still employed, employer had fewer than 51 employees on 31 December 2025
     - 30 June 2026
   * - still employed, 51 to 500 employees
     - 30 September 2026
   * - still employed, more than 500 employees
     - 31 December 2026

The record covers every pre-2026 year not yet reported, not only 2025. This module builds one calendar year at a time from that year's payslips, from 2020 on; years before your payroll ran in Odoo have to be filed from the earlier system. A generated file for 2026 or later is refused, and so is marking such a file as submitted; existing attachments are kept.

Setting it up
===============

On the company:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Value
     - Used as
   * - *Company ID* (IČO), **exactly 8 digits**
     - ``<ico>``
   * - *Sociálna poisťovňa variable symbol*, exactly 10 digits
     - ``<icz>``, the employer's identification number at the Sociálna poisťovňa
   * - city and a 5-digit ZIP (street optional)
     - the employer's address

Fill in the *Sociálna poisťovňa variable symbol* on the ELDP form itself. It is stored on the company and shared with the MVP, VPP and RLFO modules, so it is entered once; changing it needs the right to edit companies.

Per employee: the **birth number** in *Identification No*, the date of birth (*Birthday*), and the *Departure Date* of anyone who left. The **last word of the name is the surname** and everything before it the first name; with ``hr_employee_firstname`` installed, its separate name fields are used.

Preparing the ELDP
====================

*Payroll Declarations → SK ELDP (pension)*, then *New*.

1. Choose the **Year**. The period is always 1 January to 31 December.

2. Click **Generate**. Every payslip of the company whose period lies within the year is collected (cancelled ones excepted), one row is built per employee, and the XML is validated against ``ELDP-v2015_1.3.xsd`` and attached.

3. Upload the XML to the Sociálna poisťovňa portal and click **Mark Submitted**. **Reset to Draft** reopens it.

Generation stops if the company data above are incomplete, if an employee has no valid birth number or date of birth, or if the year has no payslips.

What each record contains
===========================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Item
     - Taken from
   * - birth number, surname, first name, date of birth
     - the employee
   * - start of the insurance relationship (``datVzniku``)
     - the start of the employee's current uninterrupted run of contracts (*Contract Start Date*)
   * - end (``datZaniku``) or *still lasting* (``trva``)
     - the employee's departure date, if it falls within the year
   * - period in the year (``datOd``–``datDo``)
     - the relationship clamped to the year
   * - pension assessment base (``vzDP``)
     - the sum of the employee's *GROSS* for the year
   * - type of insurance (``znakPoist``)
     - always *A* (employee)
   * - excluded days (``dniVyluc``)
     - always 0

Limitations
=============

- **The assessment base is the summed GROSS.** It is not reduced by excluded periods and no maximum is applied. Check it for employees with unpaid leave, sick leave, maternity or parental leave, and for high earners.

- **One continuous period per employee per year.** Several spells in one year (leaving and returning, an interrupted insurance) are not split into separate rows.

- Excluded days are always reported as 0, and every employee is reported as *A* (employee), including agreement workers.

- Draft payslips count; a payslip whose period crosses the year boundary is ignored.

- Only a regular filing; corrections go through the portal. Only the 2015 schema (version 1.3) is shipped.

- Schema validation checks the shape of the file, not the figures.
