Currency Rate Update SK (NBS/ECB + Slovak banks)

Slovak FX-rate providers for the OCA currency_rate_update framework, with previous-day fill

This app adds Slovak exchange-rate providers to the OCA currency_rate_update framework (res.currency.rate.provider subclasses). It is Community-compatible and does not require the Odoo Enterprise currency_rate_live feature.

The ECB provider (shipped in the OCA base) is the referenčný výmenný kurz that the National Bank of Slovakia (NBS) republishes from the European Central Bank — the rate used for statutory foreign-currency valuation in Slovak accounting. The two commercial-bank providers (VÚB banka and Tatra banka) publish their daily kurzový lístok and are intended for reconciling actual bank conversions.

Key features

  • ECB (base) — the NBS-republished ECB reference rate for statutory FX valuation.
  • VÚB banka — the bank's daily kurzový lístok (Devíza stred / middle rate).
  • Tatra banka — the bank's published exchange-rate feed.
  • Previous-day fill (default, per-provider toggle): central and commercial banks do not publish on weekends and holidays, so after each sync the last published rate is carried forward to every calendar day in the synced window. Every day then has an explicit res.currency.rate and no posting lands on a day without a rate. The fill is idempotent and never overwrites a published rate.

Note: use ECB for statutory valuation; the VÚB and Tatra banka feeds carry commercial rates for bank reconciliation only.

Usage

In Settings ▸ Technical ▸ Currency Rate Providers create a provider, pick the service (ECB, VÚB banka or Tatra banka), choose the currencies to track, leave Fill non-publishing days enabled, and run the sync (manually or via the scheduled action).

Statutory caveat (needs accountant sign-off): Slovak law values a foreign-currency transaction at the ECB reference rate of the preceding business day. Odoo returns the latest rate dated on-or-before the transaction date, so when a same-day ECB rate exists it is used instead of the prior day's. Strict alignment with the "preceding day" rule is not done here — raise it with the customer's accountant before relying on it.
