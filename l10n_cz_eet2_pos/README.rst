===============================
Point of Sale — Czech EET 2.0
===============================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Registers every paid Point of Sale order with the Czech EET 2.0 system at the moment of payment — the contact part of the payment only — prints the confirmation code on the receipt, and tells the customer when a sale will be reported later.**

What it is for
================

With this module each Point of Sale till reports its sales to the Czech **EET 2.0** system as the law requires: when the cashier validates the payment, Odoo sends the data message, waits briefly for the **confirmation code** (*POK*) and prints it on the receipt. The legal background, the company settings, certificates and the way messages are retried and stored are described in ``l10n_cz_eet2``, which this module builds on.

Setting it up
===============

1. Payment methods
--------------------

*Point of Sale → Configuration → Payment Methods*: each method has an **EET 2.0 Contact Payment** flag, on by default. Cash, card terminals and a QR code shown at the till are contact payments and stay on. Switch the flag off for a method that stands for money received remotely — a bank transfer arriving later, a payment gateway used for pick-up orders — so it is not registered.

2. The till
-------------

*Point of Sale → Configuration → Point of Sale*, open the till, tab **EET 2.0**:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - What it does
   * - EET 2.0 PoS ID (id_pokl)
     - The cash register name sent for this till, up to 20 characters; the till's name when empty. Each till of one evidence unit needs its own.
   * - EET 2.0 Unit ID (id_jednotky)
     - The evidence unit this till belongs to; the company default when empty.

The company-level switch, EIČ, environment and certificate come from the base module's settings.

What is registered
====================

An order is registered when EET 2.0 is on for the company and its payment lines made through methods flagged as contact payments add up to something other than zero. **Only that contact part is registered**, not the order total: an order paid half in cash and half through a method flagged remote reports the cash half, and an order with no contact money reports nothing and says so in its chatter. Change given back nets out.

- **Time of sale** is the time of the last payment, which is the moment the law looks at for a card or QR payment.

- **A refund** registers the negative sum of the money paid back through contact methods — the storno form the act prescribes.

- **Gift cards and e-wallets** sold or redeemed at the till are reported in the two dedicated items when ``l10n_cz_eet2_pos_loyalty`` is installed, which happens on its own as soon as the loyalty module is.

- **One message per order.** An order never carries two live sale messages; a resend reuses the one it has.

At the till and on the receipt
================================

When the cashier validates the payment, the till blocks for at most the company's **response time limit** (5 seconds by default) while the message is sent. The receipt then prints an **EET 2.0** block with the POK; on the playground it adds *NEPRODUKČNÍ / TEST*.

When the gateway does not answer in time or the till is offline, the sale still goes through and the receipt prints **Tržba bude odeslána dodatečně** — the sale will be reported later. The scheduled job keeps sending until the gateway accepts, within 48 hours and hourly after that, so nothing has to be done at the till. A message the gateway rejected for good prints nothing.

The order in the back office (*Point of Sale → Orders*) shows its **EET 2.0 Status** and **EET 2.0 POK**, and a **Resend to EET** button for an order whose message has not been accepted yet.

Limitations
=============

- The receipt carries the POK only when it arrived before printing; a receipt reprinted later shows the code the order has by then.

- Which methods are contact payments is your decision; a method flagged remote is never registered even when the customer stands at the counter.

- The till does not register sales on behalf of another taxpayer.
