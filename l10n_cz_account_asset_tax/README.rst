=====================================
Tax Depreciation — Czech localization
=====================================

Loads the six Czech depreciation groups with §31 straight-line rates, §32
accelerated coefficients and the §30a extraordinary regime, verified against the
2026 consolidated text of zákon 586/1992 Sb. Data-only; install a bridge
(``account_asset_tax_ee`` or ``account_asset_tax_oca``) to attach the board to
assets.
