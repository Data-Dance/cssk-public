---
title: SK Asset Protocols — Community (OCA asset register)
summary: Prints the Slovak asset protocols from the OCA asset register used on Odoo Community.
verified: 19.0.2.0.3
family: l10n_sk_asset_protocol_base#community
---

Adds the tab **Protokoly (SK)** to the OCA asset form and the two print entries, *Protokol o zaradení majetku* and *Protokol o vyradení majetku*, filling the protocols from the OCA register's own fields.

It installs itself when both [module:l10n_sk_asset_protocol_base](module:l10n_sk_asset_protocol_base) and OCA `account_asset_management` are installed, cannot be installed together with Enterprise `account_asset`, and has no settings.
