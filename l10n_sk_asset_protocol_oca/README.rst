=====================================================
SK Asset Protocols — Community (OCA asset register)
=====================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Prints the Slovak asset protocols from the OCA asset register used on Odoo Community.**

Adds the tab **Protokoly (SK)** to the OCA asset form and the two print entries, *Protokol o zaradení majetku* and *Protokol o vyradení majetku*, filling the protocols from the OCA register's own fields.

It installs itself when both ``l10n_sk_asset_protocol_base`` and OCA ``account_asset_management`` are installed, cannot be installed together with Enterprise ``account_asset``, and has no settings.
