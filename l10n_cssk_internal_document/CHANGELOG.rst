[19.0.1.0.1] — 2026-10-27
-------------------------

Fixed
~~~~~

- The README and code said Slovak § 10 ods. 1 zákona č. 431/2002 Z. z. asks
  the same of an accounting document as Czech § 11 odst. 1 zákona
  č. 563/1991 Sb. It does not: it has no signature of the person responsible
  for booking, and the responsible person's signature only where the case is
  not verified under § 32 ods. 3 písm. b) or c). The printout is unchanged; on
  a Slovak document its second signature line is more than the law asks.
