===============================================
SK Deferrals and Accruals (časové rozlíšenie)
===============================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Points Odoo's deferral tools at the Slovak accounts 381, 383, 384 and 385 — the OCA cut-offs on Community, Odoo's own deferrals on Enterprise.**

What it is for
================

*Časové rozlíšenie* moves a cost or revenue into the period it belongs to when the invoice falls into another one: insurance paid in advance for next year, rent received in advance, a service of December invoiced in January. The Slovak chart keeps four accounts for it:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Account
     - Slovak term
     - Gloss
     - Typical case
   * - 381
     - Náklady budúcich období
     - prepaid expense
     - insurance or rent paid in advance
   * - 383
     - Výdavky budúcich období
     - accrued expense
     - rent paid in arrears
   * - 384
     - Výnosy budúcich období
     - deferred revenue
     - rent or a subscription received in advance
   * - 385
     - Príjmy budúcich období
     - accrued revenue
     - rent received in arrears

Odoo has two different engines for this, depending on the edition. Neither knows the Slovak accounts. Two small modules connect them:

- **This module** (``l10n_sk_account_cutoff``) fills the default accounts of the **OCA cut-offs** (``account_cutoff_start_end_dates``), which run on Community and Enterprise — see `Cut-offs <#cutoffs>`__.

- ``l10n_sk_account_deferred`` fills the accounts of **Odoo Enterprise's own deferrals** — see `Enterprise deferrals <#enterprise>`__.

Both install themselves when their engine and the Slovak chart (``l10n_sk``) are present. Neither posts anything on its own: they only make the engine post to the right accounts.

Items whose amount is **not yet known** at the period end are not *časové rozlíšenie*; for those see ``l10n_cssk_accrual``.

Legal background
==================

- The `Accounting Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/431/>`__ requires items to be booked and reported in the period they relate to in time and substance — `§ 3 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/431/#paragraf-3.odsek-1>`__.

- `Opatrenie 740/2002 Z. z. <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/740/>`__ names the accounts: costs and expenditure relating to future periods are deferred on 381 and 382, or 383 (`§ 5 ods. 3 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/740/#paragraf-5.odsek-3>`__); revenue and income on 384 or 385 (`§ 5 ods. 4 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/740/#paragraf-5.odsek-4>`__). `§ 56 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2002/740/#paragraf-56>`__ describes each account — prepaid rent and subscriptions on 381, rent paid in arrears on 383, rent received in advance on 384, rent received in arrears on 385 — and lists costs that are **not** deferred, such as advertising, training and market research, unless the service itself is paid in advance (§ 56 ods. 6).

Account 382 *Komplexné náklady budúcich období* is not mapped by either module.

Cut-offs (OCA engine)
=======================

What the module sets
----------------------

On a company with the Slovak chart — when the chart is loaded, or on install for a company that already has it — the module fills, only where empty:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Setting (*Accounting → Configuration → Settings*, block *Cut-offs*)
     - Account
   * - Default Account for Prepaid Expense
     - 381000
   * - Default Account for Accrued Expenses
     - 383000
   * - Default Account for Prepaid Revenue
     - 384000
   * - Default Account for Accrued Revenues
     - 385000
   * - Default Cut-off Journal
     - the company's first *Miscellaneous* journal

It also switches **Accrual On Taxes** off. The OCA engine has it on by default and then accrues the VAT of the source lines as well, on a *Default Tax Account for Accrued Expense / Revenue* the Slovak chart does not have, so every cut-off over taxed lines stopped with *Missing 'Accrued Expense Tax Account'*. Nor would it be right: input VAT cannot be deducted before the invoice is in hand (`§ 51 ods. 1 písm. a) of the VAT Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-51.odsek-1.pismeno-a>`__), so an accrual on 383 / 385 is the amount without VAT. On install and upgrade the option is switched off only where no accrued-tax account is configured, on the company or on a tax; if your accountant set one up, it stays on.

You can change any of them there. Review two more settings of the same block, which this module leaves alone:

- **Post Cut-off Journal Entry** is off: the cut-off entry is created as a draft for you to check and post.

- **Partner on Journal Items by Default** controls whether the entry keeps the partner on each line.

Entering the dates
--------------------

The cut-off engine works from **start and end dates on invoice and bill lines** (*Start Date*, *End Date*). Enter them on every line whose cost or revenue covers a period — for example 1. 7. 2026 – 30. 6. 2027 on an annual insurance bill. To make the dates compulsory for a product, tick *Must Have Start/End Dates* on it.

Running a cut-off
-------------------

At the period end: *Accounting → Accounting → Cut-offs*, then the type, then *New*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Menu
     - Account
     - Lines it picks up
   * - Prepaid Expense
     - 381
     - bill lines dated on or before the cut-off date whose end date is after it; the part after the cut-off date is deferred
   * - Prepaid Revenue
     - 384
     - the same for invoice lines
   * - Accrued Expense
     - 383
     - bill lines dated **after** the cut-off date whose start date is on or before it; the part up to the cut-off date is brought back
   * - Accrued Revenue
     - 385
     - the same for invoice lines

The amount is split by days. Enter the cut-off date and check the source journals, then click **Re-Generate Lines**, review the lines and click **Create Journal Entry**. The entry is dated on the cut-off date.

Two practical consequences:

- 383 and 385 work only with invoices **already booked** with a later date — run them when the January invoices for December services are in, before the books are closed.

- The cut-off entry is **not reversed automatically**. Reverse it yourself as of the first day of the next period (*Reverse Entry* on the entry), so the original invoice lines carry the cost again in the period they belong to.

Only one cut-off per type and date can exist in a company.

Enterprise deferrals
======================

Odoo Enterprise (``account_accountant``) has its own deferrals: on a bill or invoice line you set the period in the **Deferred Date** column, and Odoo spreads the amount through a deferral account by month or by day.

``l10n_sk_account_deferred`` installs itself with ``account_accountant`` on a database with the Slovak chart and sets, in *Accounting → Configuration → Settings*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Setting
     - Value
   * - Deferred expense entries → Deferred expense
     - 381000
   * - Deferred revenue entries → Deferred revenue
     - 384000
   * - *Journal* of both blocks
     - the company's first *Miscellaneous* journal, if empty

Without it Enterprise fills the two accounts with the first current-asset and the first current-liability account of the chart, whatever they are. On install the module sets both accounts on every Slovak company where they are empty or still on that generic choice; an account you had set by hand is kept. Later, only new companies are touched.

Whether entries are generated when the bill is posted (*On bill validation*) or in one batch (*Manually & Grouped*), and whether the amount is split by months or days, are Enterprise's own settings in the same blocks. The deferral reports are under *Accounting → Review → Regularization Entries → Deferred Expenses / Deferred Revenues* (in Slovak the middle menu reads *Regulatívne požiadavky*, a mistranslation).

Enterprise deferrals cover only the in-advance cases, 381 and 384. Accrued items on 383 and 385 need the cut-offs above or a manual entry.

Limitations
=============

- Both modules only set default accounts and journals. They neither compute nor post anything themselves.

- Accrued items are booked without VAT. For a company that is not a VAT payer, whose VAT is a cost, add the VAT to the accrual by hand. For goods acquired from another EU member state the tax is due in the period of the taxable supply, but in practice it is booked with the invoice; when the invoice arrives late, a supplementary VAT return for the right period declares both the tax and its deduction (see ``l10n_cssk_accrual``).

- Cut-off entries are not reversed automatically.

- Odoo's Slovak chart names account 385000 *Náklady budúcich období (krátkodobé)* and 383000 *Výdaje budúcich období*; the opatrenie calls them *Príjmy budúcich období* and *Výdavky budúcich období*. The accounts are the right ones; only the names shipped by Odoo are off. Rename them in the chart of accounts if they confuse your users.

- On Enterprise both engines can be installed at the same time, and an invoice line then shows both sets of dates. Use one engine for a given line: dates entered in both would defer the amount twice.

- Neither module checks whether a cost may be deferred at all (§ 56 ods. 6 of the opatrenie).
