======================================
Signed Amounts on Credit Notes (PDF)
======================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Prints the amounts on a customer credit note as negative numbers, the way Czech and Slovak readers expect a credit note to look.**

What it is for
================

Odoo prints a customer credit note with positive amounts and relies on the title to say that the money goes the other way. Czech and Slovak accountants and customers expect a credit note (*dobropis*, *opravný daňový doklad*) to show the reduction as **negative** amounts. This module changes the printed document accordingly. It changes nothing in the accounting.

What changes on the PDF
=========================

On a **customer credit note**, these values are printed with a minus sign:

- the unit price, and the subtotal (or total, if your prices include tax) of each line;

- the subtotal of each section;

- lines grouped on the document;

- the totals block: the tax base, the tax per tax group and the total, also in the company currency when the credit note is in a foreign currency.

Customer invoices, vendor bills and **vendor credit notes** keep their signs. The amount due, the list of payments and the payment QR code are not changed. There is nothing to configure: install the module and print.

Limitations
=============

- Only customer credit notes are affected; vendor credit notes printed from Odoo stay positive.

- The negative unit price is the module's choice of presentation: the quantity stays positive.
