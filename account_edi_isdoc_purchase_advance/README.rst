===================================
ISDOC — Received Advance Invoices
===================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Imported ISDOC proformas create a received advance instead of a bill to be posted, and the supplier's tax documents for your payment go to the advance journal and accounts, linked to their advance.**

Routes imported advance documents into ``purchase_order_advance_invoice``. A **proforma** (type 4) creates a received advance over the document's gross amount, and the draft bill the import made is flagged as not to be posted. The same proforma imported again — same supplier, same supplier's number, advance not cancelled — creates no second advance: the new draft is linked to the existing one. A **tax document for your payment** (type 5, credit note 6) is moved to the received advance tax documents journal, takes its taxable supply date from the file, has its lines moved to the paid-advances account and the payable to the clearing account, and is linked to the matching open advance — by order reference, variable symbol or unique paid amount; when that is ambiguous, the chatter asks you to link it by hand. It adds the product *Advance payment (imported)*.

It installs itself when ``account_edi_isdoc`` and ``purchase_order_advance_invoice`` are both installed; the journal and accounts are those set up for received advances.
