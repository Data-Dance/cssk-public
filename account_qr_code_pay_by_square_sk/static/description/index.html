PAY by square (Slovakia)

The Slovak PAY by square payment QR code on your invoices — generated fully offline

This app adds the Slovak PAY by square payment QR code to Odoo invoices. PAY by square is the Slovak Banking Association standard supported by every Slovak banking app: the customer scans the code printed on the invoice and the payment form is pre-filled with the IBAN, amount, currency, beneficiary and message.

The code is built offline — the PAY by square sequence (CRC32 checksum, LZMA1/XZ compression, base32 encoding) is produced entirely in Python with no external service. It registers as an extra payment-QR method on res.partner.bank and reuses Odoo's standard QR-on-invoice plumbing, so it runs on both Community and Enterprise.

Key features

  • New PAY by square (Slovakia) payment-QR method on every bank account.
  • Pure-offline generator per the SBA PAY by square specification 1.1.0 — no network dependency.
  • Encodes IBAN, amount, currency, beneficiary name and the payment message (variable symbol / structured reference) from the invoice.
  • Branded QR frame on the printed invoice.
  • Eligibility checks: EUR currency, IBAN account in a SEPA country, and a beneficiary name present.

Usage

Settings ▸ Invoicing ▸ Customer Payments — enable QR Codes. On a customer invoice, open Other Info ▸ Payment QR-code and pick PAY by square (Slovakia). The QR code is rendered on the invoice PDF; the customer scans it in their banking app to pay.
