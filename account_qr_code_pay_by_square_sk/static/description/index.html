Account QR Code Pay by Square SK

The module adds Slovakia Pay By Square QR Code to reports.
It is an offline generator and does not depend on external services.

Table of contents

  • Usage
  • Author
  • Contact

Usage

  1. Go to Settings > Invoicing > Customer Payments
  2. Enable QR codes
  3. Go to any invoice Other info > Payment QR-code > PAY by square (Slovakia)
