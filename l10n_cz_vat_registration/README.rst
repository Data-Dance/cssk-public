==============================================================
CZ VAT Registration Category (plátce / identifikovaná osoba)
==============================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Records whether a partner's Czech DIČ belongs to a plátce, a skupina or an identifikovaná osoba, and warns when a domestic reverse charge is aimed at a customer who is not a plátce.**

What it is for
================

A Czech DIČ does not mean the same thing for everyone who holds one. An **identifikovaná osoba** (identified person) is registered for VAT because it buys goods from another member state, receives services from abroad or supplies services to another member state. It has a valid DIČ, which VIES confirms — but it is **not a plátce** (VAT payer). It does not charge VAT on domestic supplies, does not deduct input VAT, and the domestic reverse charge (*režim přenesení daňové povinnosti*) cannot be applied to it.

VIES answers only valid / not valid. This module adds the kind of registration to the contact and warns when an invoice applies the domestic reverse charge to a customer who is not a plátce.

Your own company's status, and how it changes over time, is the subject of ``l10n_cz_vat_status``. This module is about your customers and suppliers.

Legal background
==================

All references are to the `zákon o DPH (č. 235/2004 Sb.) <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235>`__.

- `§ 6 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235#p6>`__ to § 6f — who is a **plátce**: a taxable person established in Czechia from 1 January of the year after its turnover exceeded CZK 2,000,000 (or earlier, on exceeding CZK 2,536,500 or by choice), and in the other cases of § 6b to § 6f, including voluntary registration.

- `§ 5a <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235#p5a>`__ — a **skupina** (VAT group) comes into being by being registered as a plátce, and acts as one.

- `§ 6g <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235#p6g>`__ to § 6l — who is an **identifikovaná osoba**: a person who is not a plátce and acquires goods from another member state, receives a taxable supply from a person not established in Czechia, or supplies a service with its place of supply in another member state; § 6j to § 6l cover registration by choice.

- `§ 92a odst. 1 <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2004/235#p92a>`__ — in the režim přenesení daňové povinnosti the tax is accounted for by the **plátce for whom the supply was made**. A customer who is not a plátce cannot take the tax over, so the supplier charges and pays Czech VAT as usual.

Setting it up
===============

Nothing to configure. On the contact form, below the DIČ (*Tax ID*), two fields appear for Czech partners and partners without a country:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - Meaning
   * - Registrace k DPH
     - Plátce, Skupina (a plátce), Identifikovaná osoba (not a plátce) or Neplátce
   * - Registrace k DPH od
     - the day the registration took effect (shown once the category is set); entered by hand

Fill the category in by hand, or let ``l10n_cz_payment_reliability`` fill it: select one or more contacts and run *Actions → Check tax reliability*. Besides the reliability rating, the action then reads from the Finanční správa register whether each Czech contact is a plátce, a skupina or an identifikovaná osoba. When the register does not find the DIČ, or lists the subject as a nespolehlivá osoba, the field is **left as it was**, never set to "not a plátce".

The warning on invoices
=========================

On a customer invoice of a Czech company a yellow warning appears at the top, in Czech with an English translation, when all of these hold:

- the customer's category is **Identifikovaná osoba** or **Neplátce**;

- the customer's country is Czechia, or not set;

- a line carries a tax that marks a domestic reverse charge.

A tax marks a domestic reverse charge when it reports its base on **row 25 of the VAT return** (the *VAT 25* tax grid, as on the *0% RC* tax of Odoo's Czech localization), or when it has the *CZ Reverse Charge phrase (§92a)* box of ``l10n_cz_invoice`` ticked. A tax with neither is not recognised, and the check stays silent.

When the invoice is posted the same text is written to its chatter. The warning **never blocks** posting: correct the taxes yourself — the supply is taxed with ordinary Czech VAT.

Limitations
=============

- **The category is only as current as its last update.** Nothing refreshes it on a schedule; a customer who becomes a plátce keeps its old category until you or the register lookup change it.

- **Only the domestic reverse charge is checked.** Other consequences of the customer's status (for example on intra-Community supplies or on the kontrolní hlášení) are not.

- **§ 92a has more conditions than the customer being a plátce** — the kind of supply, for one. The module checks none of them.

- **Registrace k DPH od** is not available from the register and stays empty unless you enter it.

- A member of a skupina uses the group's DIČ; record the group, not the member's former registration.

- Vendor bills are not checked.
