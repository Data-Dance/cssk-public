==================================
Payment Mode — Pay on Validation
==================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**A payment mode can register the payment the moment the invoice is posted, for sales paid in cash or by card on the spot.**

What it is for
================

When a customer pays in cash or by card at the counter, the invoice is paid the moment it is issued. Registering the payment as a separate step is only a chance to forget it, and the open invoice then shows up in the receivables and in payment reminders.

This module adds one option to OCA's payment modes (``account_payment_mode``): an invoice whose payment mode has it is **paid in full automatically when it is posted**, in the journal the payment mode names.

Setting it up
===============

*Accounting → Configuration → Payment Modes* (the module has no Slovak or Czech translation, so its labels are in English). Create or open a payment mode, for example *Cash* or *Card*:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - Value
   * - Payment Type
     - Inbound for customer invoices
   * - Link to Bank Account
     - **Fixed**
   * - Fixed Bank Journal
     - the journal the money arrives in — a cash journal for cash, the card clearing journal for cards
   * - **Register payment on validation**
     - ticked

The checkbox appears only when *Link to Bank Account* is *Fixed*, and a mode with it ticked cannot be saved without a fixed journal.

Set the mode on the invoice (field *Payment Mode*), or as the customer's default payment mode on the contact so that new invoices get it automatically.

What happens when you post
============================

When an invoice is posted and its payment mode has **Register payment on validation**:

- a payment for the **whole amount still due** is created and reconciled with the invoice, through Odoo's standard *Register Payment* wizard — the same payment as if you had registered it by hand;

- in the payment mode's fixed journal;

- dated the **invoice date** (the accounting date if there is none).

The invoice then shows as *Paid*, or *In Payment* if the journal uses an outstanding receipts account and waits for the bank statement.

It works the same for customer credit notes, vendor bills and receipts that carry such a payment mode. An invoice that is already paid, or has nothing left to pay, is left alone. Several invoices posted together each get their own payment.

Limitations
=============

- The payment is always for the full amount. For a partly paid sale, use a payment mode without the option and register the payment yourself.

- The payment is dated the invoice date, not the moment of posting. If you post an invoice dated in the past, the payment is dated in the past too.

- If the payment cannot be created — for example the journal has no suitable payment method — the invoice is not posted either.

- The module only records the payment. It does not issue a cash register receipt or talk to a card terminal.
