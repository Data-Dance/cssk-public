=================================
Payment Mode: Pay on Validation
=================================

A payment mode (OCA ``account_payment_mode``) with a fixed journal can
register the payment of an invoice the moment the invoice is posted — for
sales paid in cash or by card on the spot, where a separate payment step is
only a chance to forget it.

Configuration
=============

On the payment mode: *Link to Bank Account* = Fixed, choose the cash or card
journal, tick *Register payment on validation*.
