==============================================
Additional-income tax and health calculation
==============================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Reviewed monthly cash settlement from historical social premiums and dated tax/health parameters, before native payroll integration.**

Purpose
=========

Calculate reviewed positive cash remuneration after termination. Social premiums come from the historical register; health and income tax use the reviewed reporting month and native dated parameters. Other monthly payments are consolidated before applying tax bands/NČZD. The one-day 2Y health profile supports minimum top-ups and refund of a previous monthly top-up.

Editable form fields and optional hidden API facts cannot be mixed. Evidence, source/parameter digests, archived calculations, retries and reset dependencies are checked. The calculation stores amounts, not an accounting entry.

Limits
========

Monthly cash settlement paid in the next month of the same year, insurance ended before that reporting month, only one 2Y health day; no child bonus, other benefits/deductions or exceptional corrections. Health profiles and tax pension status are separate reviewed facts. Native slip/accounting integration belongs to the separate native payroll bridge; historical VPP/health exports remain separate.

Legal basis
=============

Monthly tax consolidation follows `§ 35 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-35>`__, declaration facts `§ 36 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-36>`__, and cent rounding `§ 47 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-47>`__. The pension status at 1 January under `§11(6) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-11.odsek-6>`__ does not grant a monthly allowance merely because the declaration checkbox is selected. Finančná správa's `2026 NČZD FAQ, questions 7 and 9 <https://podpora.financnasprava.sk/260857-Nezdanite%C4%BEn%C3%A1-%C4%8Das%C5%A5-z%C3%A1kladu-dane-na-da%C5%88ovn%C3%ADka-za-rok-2026>`__ explicitly excludes monthly NČZD for that pension profile, including a small pension; the difference after annual pension including the 13th pension belongs to annual reconciliation/personal return. A pension first effective later in the year is a different tax fact. The 2027 §35 wording retains that distinction.

Health scope/exclusions follow `§ 10b <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-10b>`__ and `§11(3) <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-11.odsek-3>`__, rates and monthly advances `§ 16 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-16>`__, and the day-proportional minimum, its exceptions and additional employee advance `§ 16a <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/580/#paragraf-16a>`__. The 2Y code and last-day, single-day registration follow insurer methodology, not a literal code in the statute: `Union instructions <https://www.union.sk/pomoc-a-podpora/nepravidelny-prijem-a-prijem-vyplateny-zamestnancovi-po-skonceni-pracovneho-pomeru/>`__. If an earlier payment of the same month already charged a minimum top-up, the new calculation pays only the difference from the consolidated monthly amount; a negative difference returns the earlier excess. It is not an annual-insurer refund or permission to refund an unrelated period's contribution.
