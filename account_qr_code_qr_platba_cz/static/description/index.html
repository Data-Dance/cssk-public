QR Platba (Czech Republic)

The Czech QR Platba payment QR code on your invoices — generated fully offline

This app adds the Czech QR Platba payment QR code to Odoo invoices. QR Platba is the Czech Banking Association short-payment-descriptor (SPD) standard supported by every Czech banking app: the customer scans the code printed on the invoice and the payment form is pre-filled with the IBAN, amount, currency, message and the variable symbol.

The code is built offline — the SPD string (SPD*1.0*ACC:...*AM:...*CC:...) is assembled directly from the invoice data and rendered as a QR code, with no external service. It registers as an extra payment-QR method on res.partner.bank and reuses Odoo's standard QR-on-invoice plumbing, so it runs on both Community and Enterprise.

Key features

  • New QR Platba (Czech Republic) payment-QR method on every bank account.
  • Pure-offline SPD generator per the CBA QR Platba standard 1.2 — no network dependency.
  • Encodes IBAN, amount, currency, message and the variable-symbol reference (digits-only RF field) from the invoice.
  • Branded QR frame on the printed invoice.
  • Eligibility checks: CZK currency or a Czech debtor, IBAN account in a SEPA country, and a beneficiary name present.

Usage

Settings ▸ Invoicing ▸ Customer Payments — enable QR Codes. On a customer invoice, open Other Info ▸ Payment QR-code and pick QR Platba (Czech Republic). The QR code is rendered on the invoice PDF; the customer scans it in their banking app to pay.
