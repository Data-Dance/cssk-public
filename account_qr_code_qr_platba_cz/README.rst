============================
QR Platba (Czech Republic)
============================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Prints the Czech QR Platba payment code on customer invoices, with the variable, constant and specific symbols as separate values.**

What it is for
================

**QR Platba** is the payment QR code standard of the Czech Banking Association (*Česká bankovní asociace*), built on the Short Payment Descriptor (SPD) format. Every Czech banking app reads it: the customer scans the code on your invoice and gets a payment order with your account, the amount and the variable symbol filled in.

This module adds QR Platba as a QR format for Odoo invoices. What the national formats have in common — switching QR codes on, the bank account they need and how Odoo picks a format for each invoice — is described in ``account_qr_code_frame_provider``, installed with this module.

The code is generated offline, entirely on your server; no external service is called.

Setting it up
===============

1. Switch on *QR Codes* in *Accounting → Configuration → Settings* — see the common setup.

2. Enter the bank account in **IBAN** form (for example ``CZ65 0800 …``), not as a domestic number with a bank code, and fill in the *Account Holder Name*. The IBAN must be from a SEPA country.

QR Platba comes **first** in Odoo's automatic choice and is used for every invoice that is **in CZK, or addressed to a customer in the Czech Republic** in any currency. Other invoices fall through to the next format. You can always pick *QR Platba (Czech Republic)* — the label is shown in English — in the invoice's *Payment QR-code* field yourself; see Which format an invoice gets.

What the code contains
========================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - SPD field
     - Taken from
   * - ``ACC`` account
     - the IBAN of the invoice's *Recipient Bank* account
   * - ``AM`` amount, ``CC`` currency
     - the amount still open on the invoice, in the invoice currency
   * - ``PT`` payment type
     - always ``IP``: the banking app is asked to send an instant payment, so the money usually arrives within seconds; a bank that cannot falls back to an ordinary transfer
   * - ``MSG`` message for the payee
     - the *Payment Reference*, or the invoice number when there is none; first 60 characters, with ``*`` written as ``%2A`` and ``%`` as ``%25`` as the standard prescribes
   * - ``RF`` payee's reference
     - the variable symbol, or else the digits of the message; at most 16 digits
   * - ``RN`` payee's name
     - *Account Holder Name*, or the name of the account's owner; escaped like ``MSG``
   * - ``X-VS``, ``X-SS``, ``X-KS``
     - the invoice's variable, specific and constant symbols, with ``l10n_cssk_payment_symbols`` installed

The code is drawn inside the QR Platba frame.

Limitations
=============

- No due date (``DT``) is passed; the banking app proposes its own payment date.

- QR Platba is chosen for any invoice to a Czech customer, also in euro and also when your company is Slovak. Pick another format on the invoice if you do not want that.

- Without the payment symbols module there are no ``X-VS``/``X-SS``/``X-KS`` fields; the variable symbol then reaches the customer only through ``RF`` and the message, if the reference contains it.
