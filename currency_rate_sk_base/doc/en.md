title: Slovak Bank Exchange Rates — Shared Download
summary: Downloads and reads the VÚB banka and Tatra banka exchange-rate lists for the Slovak rate providers.
verified: 19.0.1.0.2
family: currency_rate_update_sk#bank-rates
---

Downloads and reads the exchange-rate lists that VÚB banka (the middle rate for transfers, *Devíza stred*) and Tatra banka publish on their websites, as euro-based rates. It has no screen, settings or data of its own. The services built on it are described with the Slovak exchange-rate providers.

It installs itself as a dependency of [module:currency_rate_update_sk](module:currency_rate_update_sk); there is no reason to install it on its own.
