============================
SK Tax Depreciation Groups
============================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The seven Slovak tax depreciation groups (odpisové skupiny 0–6) with their periods and accelerated coefficients, for the tax depreciation board.**

What it is for
================

Loads the Slovak statutory depreciation groups (*odpisové skupiny*) into the tax depreciation module, ``account_asset_tax``. With them, a Slovak company can put an asset into a group and have its tax depreciation board (*daňové odpisy*) computed by the Slovak rules: monthly pro-rating in the first year, the unclaimed remainder in the year after the period, and accelerated depreciation for groups 2 and 3.

How the board is computed, and everything you do with it, is described in the tax depreciation guide.

Legal background
==================

`Act No. 595/2003 Z. z. on income tax <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/>`__:

- the groups and periods are in `§ 26 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-26.odsek-1>`__; which group an asset belongs to is set by annex 1 to the act, by production classification;

- accelerated depreciation is allowed only in groups 2 and 3, and the method cannot change during the asset's depreciation — `§ 26 ods. 3 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-26.odsek-3>`__;

- straight-line annual charge = entry price ÷ period, with the first year pro-rated by months — `§ 27 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-27.odsek-1>`__;

- the accelerated coefficients — `§ 28 ods. 1 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-28.odsek-1>`__.

The records are named *Odpisová skupina 0* to *6* with codes ``SK-0`` to ``SK-6``, and appear in the asset's group field as, for example, ``SK-2 — Odpisová skupina 2 (6 y)``.

Setting it up
===============

Install it with the tax depreciation module and a bridge to your asset register (``account_asset_tax_oca`` on Community, ``account_asset_tax_ee`` on Enterprise). It requires Odoo's Slovak localization (``l10n_sk``). There is nothing to configure: the groups are visible under *Accounting → Configuration → Tax Depreciation Groups*, and the asset form offers them to companies whose country is Slovakia. The values are reloaded every time the module is updated, so changes made by hand do not survive an update.

Passenger vehicles: the VAT outside the entry price
=====================================================

From 1 January 2026 a payer deducts only half the VAT on a passenger vehicle used also privately (`§ 85n of the VAT Act <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-85n>`__). The books capitalise the other half into the vehicle's acquisition cost, but for income tax it "nie je súčasťou vstupnej ceny" (`§ 52zzzk <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2003/595/#paragraf-52zzzk>`__).

With ``l10n_sk_vehicle_expense`` installed, the tax entry price the board proposes for such an asset leaves it out: the asset is valued at what its bill lines put on the asset account, and the board starts from that minus the undeducted VAT of the taxes flagged *Vehicle VAT limited by § 85n*. A car bought for 20 000 € plus 23 % VAT with the vehicle purchase tax is an asset of 22 300 € and gets a tax entry price of 20 000 €. The difference between accounting and tax depreciation reaches the corporate income tax return through its Table B.

The entry price is only proposed — when you switch tax depreciation on, or when the board is computed with the entry price empty. A price you enter yourself is kept. Where one bill line becomes several assets, each takes its share of the VAT.

Limitations
=============

- Only the groups are loaded. Classifying an asset into a group is your decision; nothing suggests a group from the product or the account.

- No straight-line rates are stored: the board derives the annual charge from the period.

- Where the board departs from the act or does not cover it — the disposal year, cars from 48 000 €, the micro-taxpayer regime — is listed in the tax depreciation guide.

- Intangible assets have no depreciation group: the Act classifies only tangible assets, so an empty group on an intangible asset is correct and final.
