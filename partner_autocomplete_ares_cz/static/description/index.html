Partner Autocomplete using ARES CZ

Complete Czech partner data from the official ARES business register

Completes partner information from the Czech ARES business register (ares.gov.cz, formerly wwwinfo.mfcr.cz). It plugs into the partner_autocomplete_dispatcher framework as the ARES.CZ provider, so it can be selected per company and used for type-ahead lookup, single-record enrichment and bulk import enrichment.

Lookups are by company name (search) or by IČO (company registration number); the result fills name, address, country and DIČ (tax ID), and can map the IČO into a field of your choice via a configurable dynamic mapping.

Key features

  • Type-ahead autocomplete by company name, returning matching Czech entities with their IČO and seat.
  • Enrichment by IČO: fills name, street, ZIP, city, country and DIČ from the ARES economic-subject record.
  • Configurable dynamic field mapping — e.g. write the IČO into the company registration field of your choice.
  • Bulk enrichment during data import (an import_enrich_company column), and on-demand Update from the contact list action.

Usage

In Settings ▸ General Settings ▸ Contacts enable Partner Autocomplete, choose the ARES.CZ provider and (optionally) the field for the IČO mapping. On a contact, start typing a company name or IČO and pick a suggestion; the record is filled from ARES. To update existing records, select them in the Contacts list and run Action ▸ Update. To enrich on import, add an import_enrich_company column with the IČO.
