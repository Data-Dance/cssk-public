# Czech Republic Payroll — Legal Reference (2025 & 2026)

> Scope: monthly payroll of an employed person (zaměstnanec) in the Czech Republic.
> Compiled 2026-07-04. Figures labelled **[2026 confirmed]** are set by regulation in force; **[2025]** are prior-year values kept for comparison / historical payslips.
> Primary sources: ČSSZ (Česká správa sociálního zabezpečení), Finanční správa, MPSV, VZP; secondary: KPMG/EY/Accace/TPA and specialised portals. URLs are cited inline.

The 2024–2025 **konsolidační balíček** (consolidation package) drove the material changes this document captures:
- employee **sickness insurance 0.6 %** reintroduced from 1.1.2024 (employee social went 6.5 % → 7.1 %);
- new **DPP notification / "oznámená dohoda"** regime and DPP insurance threshold tied to 25 % of the average wage;
- personal income-tax 23 % band threshold lowered to **36× average wage** (from 48×) effective 2024;
- abolition of statutory **zaručená mzda** in the private sector from 2025 (only public-sector *zaručený plat* remains).

The single most important derived input is the **average wage (průměrná mzda)**, which drives the social-security cap, the tax-band threshold and the DPP threshold:
- **[2026 confirmed] průměrná mzda = 48 967 Kč/měsíc** (gov. reg. **č. 365/2025 Sb.**). Source: https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026 ; https://www.ey.com/cs_cz/technical/tax/tax-alerts/2025/10/nova-vyse-prumerne-mzdy-pro-rok-2026
- **[2025] průměrná mzda = 46 557 Kč/měsíc**. Source: https://asbgroup.eu/cs/novinky/prumerna-mzda-pro-rok-2025/

## 1. Social security — Sociální (pojistné na sociální zabezpečení), ČSSZ

Base = **hrubá mzda** (gross wage; same base as tax since superhrubá mzda was abolished). Insurance covers three sub-components: **důchodové** (pension), **nemocenské** (sickness), **státní politika zaměstnanosti** (state employment policy / unemployment).

### Rates

| Payer | Total | Pension (důchodové) | Sickness (nemocenské) | Unemployment (st. pol. zaměstnanosti) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Employee** | **7.1 %** | 6.5 % | **0.6 %** (reintroduced 1.1.2024) | 0 % |
| **Employer** (standard) | **24.8 %** | 21.5 % | 2.1 % | 1.2 % |

Rates are unchanged 2025→2026. Sources: https://www.cssz.gov.cz/vyse-a-sazba ; https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026 ; https://mpsv.gov.cz/socialni-pojisteni

Employer variants exist (higher pension component): healthcare/rescue workers 29.8 %, hazardous occupations 27.8 % — out of scope for a v1 core module but note-worthy.

There is a **pojistné sleva for working pensioners** ("pracující důchodce") = **6.5 %** of the employee's assessment base, reducing the employee's own contribution. Source: https://www.podnikatel.cz/clanky/zmeny-v-socialnim-pojisteni-v-roce-2026-v-oblasti-zamestnavani/

### Maximální vyměřovací základ (annual cap) = 48× average wage

Applies to the **employee's own** social contribution and to the employer's; once the *annual* cumulative base is reached, no further social insurance is due for the rest of the calendar year. **Health insurance has NO cap.**

| Year | Max annual social base (= 48× avg wage) |
|---|---|
| **[2026 confirmed]** | **2 350 416 Kč** |
| **[2025]** | 2 234 736 Kč |

Sources: https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026 ; https://portal.pohoda.cz/dane-ucetnictvi-mzdy/mzdy-a-prace/vyssi-prumerna-mzda-od-1-1-2026-nove-hranice-a-limity/

### Participation threshold (rozhodný příjem) — small-scale employment / DPČ

Minimum monthly income at which an employment (incl. DPČ) becomes insured:
- **[2026 confirmed] 4 500 Kč** (i.e. ≥ 4 500 → insured). Source: https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026
- **[2025] 4 000 Kč**. Source: https://www.pruvodcepodnikanim.cz/clanek/minimalni-prumerna-zarucena-mzda-2025/

## 2. Health insurance — Zdravotní pojištění (zdravotní pojišťovny, e.g. VZP)

Base = gross wage. **No maximum cap.**

| Payer | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | **4.5 %** |
| **Employer** | **9.0 %** |
| **Total** | **13.5 %** |

Source: https://www.vzp.cz/platci/informace/zamestnavatel/vymerovaci-zaklad-a-vypocet-pojistneho

### Minimální vyměřovací základ = minimum wage (employer top-up-to-minimum rule)

For an employee the **minimum monthly health-insurance base equals the minimum wage**. If actual gross < minimum wage (e.g. part-time), health insurance is still computed from the **minimum wage**, and the **shortfall (13.5 % of the difference) is paid by the EMPLOYEE** (deducted on top of the normal 4.5 %). The employer only bears its 9 % of the actual wage. Source: https://www.vzp.cz/platci/informace/zamestnavatel/vymerovaci-zaklad-a-vypocet-pojistneho/jaky-je-minimalni-vymerovaci-zaklad

| Year | Min wage = min HI base | Min monthly HI premium (13.5 %) |
|---|---|---|
| **[2026 confirmed]** | **22 400 Kč** | **3 024 Kč** |
| **[2025]** | 20 800 Kč | 2 808 Kč |

Source: https://www.vzp.cz/o-nas/tiskove-centrum/otazky-tydne/platby-zdravotniho-pojisteni-v-roce-2026

### State-insured persons (státní pojištěnci)

The minimum-base rule **does not apply** to defined categories (state pays for them): pensioners, students, persons on parental leave, registered jobseekers, persons with disabilities (poživatelé invalidního důchodu), etc. For such an employee no top-up to the minimum is required.
- **[2026 confirmed]** state assessment base **16 206 Kč** → state pays **2 188 Kč**/month per insured person. Source: https://www.vzp.cz/o-nas/tiskove-centrum/otazky-tydne/platby-zdravotniho-pojisteni-v-roce-2026

## 3. Income tax — Daň z příjmů ze závislé činnosti (Finanční správa)

- **Superhrubá mzda abolished since 2021** → tax base = **gross wage** (rounded up: monthly advance base rounded up to whole 100 Kč for the calculation; per §38h).
- **Two bands** on the monthly advance:
  - **15 %** up to **3× average wage per month**;
  - **23 %** on the part above that (the annual equivalent is **36× average wage**).

| Year | Monthly 23 % threshold (3× avg wage) | Annual 23 % threshold (36× avg wage) |
|---|---|---|
| **[2026 confirmed]** | **146 901 Kč** | **1 762 812 Kč** |
| **[2025]** | 139 671 Kč | 1 676 052 Kč |

Sources: https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/financni-sprava/media-a-verejnost/tiskove-zpravy-gfr/tiskove-zpravy-2026/danove-novinky-pro-rok-2026 ; https://www.ey.com/cs_cz/technical/tax/tax-alerts/2024/09/zmeny-v-danovych-limitech-pro-rok-2025

### Monthly advance computation (záloha na daň), high level
1. `base = gross` (rounded up to whole 100 Kč, per §38h ZDP).
2. `advance = 15% * min(base, monthly_threshold) + 23% * max(base - monthly_threshold, 0)`.
3. Subtract monthly **slevy na dani** (tax credits) — only if the employee has signed **prohlášení poplatníka** (taxpayer declaration) with this employer → `advance_after_credits = max(advance - credits, 0)`.
4. Subtract monthly **daňové zvýhodnění na děti**; if it exceeds the tax after credits, the negative part becomes **daňový bonus** (paid out), subject to eligibility.
5. If no prohlášení signed **and** monthly income is low, a **srážková daň** (15 % withholding, final) regime may apply for certain small incomes / DPP — see §6.

### Daňové zvýhodnění na děti (child tax benefit) — tiered by child order

| Child order | Annual | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| 1st child | **15 204 Kč** | **1 267 Kč** |
| 2nd child | **22 320 Kč** | **1 860 Kč** |
| 3rd and each further | **27 840 Kč** | **2 320 Kč** |

Doubled if the child holds a **ZTP/P** card. Unchanged 2025 & 2026. Source: https://www.pruvodcepodnikanim.cz/clanek/danovy-bonus-na-dite-v-roce-2026/ ; https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/dane/dane/dan-z-prijmu/zamestnanci-zamestnavatele/obecne-informace

### Daňový bonus (payable when child benefit > tax)

- If child benefit exceeds the tax after credits, the surplus is paid to the employee as a **daňový bonus**.
- **Monthly bonus** is paid only if monthly income ≥ **½ × minimum wage** (2026: ≥ 11 200 Kč; 2025: ≥ 10 400 Kč).
- **Annual bonus** requires yearly income ≥ **6× minimum wage** (2026: ≥ 134 400 Kč).
- The **monthly bonus cap of 5 025 Kč was abolished** (consolidation package) — no upper monthly cap now.
Source: https://www.pruvodcepodnikanim.cz/clanek/danovy-bonus-na-dite-v-roce-2026/

## 5. Non-taxable / benefit items relevant to monthly payroll

- **Stravenkový paušál / meal allowance (stravné):** employer contribution tax-exempt (income tax + social + health) up to **70 % of the upper limit of stravné for a 5–12 h business trip**.
  - **[2026 confirmed]** limit **129,50 Kč/směna** (70 % of 185 Kč). Condition: shift ≥ 3 h.
  - **[2025]** limit **123,90 Kč/směna** (70 % of 177 Kč).
  - Excess over the limit is taxable and subject to insurance. Source: https://www.accace.cz/prispevek-na-stravovani/ ; https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/novy-limit-stravneho-kolik-mohou-firmy-prispet-na-stravenku-bez-zdaneni/
- **Employee-benefit annual exemption** (leisure/health "volnočasové benefity") — exempt up to ½ average wage per year (2026 ≈ 24 483 Kč); a separate higher health-oriented sublimit exists. Out of scope for v1 monthly engine but relevant for advanced modules.
- **Pension/life insurance contributions by employer** exempt up to 50 000 Kč/yr — out of v1 scope.

## 6. Dohody — DPP & DPČ

### DPP — Dohoda o provedení práce
- Max **300 hours/year** per employer.
- **Insurance threshold tied to 25 % of average wage**, rounded down to nearest 500 Kč:
  - **[2026 confirmed] 12 000 Kč**: gross ≤ 11 999 Kč → **no** social/health insurance; ≥ 12 000 Kč → insured.
  - **[2025] 11 500 Kč**.
- **"Oznámená dohoda" / notification regime (from 2024/2025):** employer must **register/notify every DPP to ČSSZ** (report the agreement and monthly income). A single **notified** DPP per employee enjoys the higher 25 %-of-average-wage threshold; a non-notified DPP (or a second concurrent DPP) is insured under the general lower threshold. From **April 2026** DPPs feed the new monthly reporting **JMHZ** system. Sources: https://www.pruvodcepodnikanim.cz/clanek/dohody-o-provedeni-prace-dpp-2026/ ; https://www.finance.cz/clanky/553494-prace-na-dohody-dpp-dpc-v-roce-2026-jak-se-zmeni-limity-a-zdaneni-prace-na-dohodu/
- **Tax:** below the threshold and without a signed prohlášení, income up to the DPP limit is taxed by **15 % srážková daň** (final withholding); otherwise normal advance with credits.

### DPČ — Dohoda o pracovní činnosti
- Insurance threshold = general **rozhodný příjem**: **[2026 confirmed] 4 500 Kč** (≥ → insured); **[2025] 4 000 Kč**.
- Average work ≤ ½ of standard weekly hours over the agreement's term.
Source: https://www.idoklad.cz/blog/dohody-o-praci-dpp-dpc-jejich-limity-i-vyhody ; https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026

## 7. Sickness — Náhrada mzdy (first 14 days) + Nemocenská

- **Days 1–14 of temporary incapacity (dočasná pracovní neschopnost):** paid by the **employer** as **náhrada mzdy**, only for **missed shifts/working days** (not calendar days). No waiting period (karenční doba abolished 2019).
- **From day 15:** **nemocenská** paid by ČSSZ from sickness insurance.
- **Náhrada mzdy = 60 % of the reduced average hourly earnings** (redukovaný průměrný hodinový výdělek). The average hourly earnings from the previous quarter are reduced at three **redukční hranice** (hourly = daily sickness thresholds × 0.175):
  - Derived from the daily sickness reduction thresholds **[2026 confirmed]** 1st **1 633 Kč**, 2nd **2 449 Kč**, 3rd **4 897 Kč** (source: https://www.cssz.gov.cz/-/prehled-nejdulezitejsich-udaju-pro-socialni-zabezpeceni-v-roce-2026 ).
  - Hourly earnings above ≈ **814,80 Kč** are ignored; max náhrada per 40 h ≈ **10 756 Kč** [2026]. Source: https://www.mesec.cz/kalkulacky/nemocenske-nahrada-mzdy/
- Náhrada mzdy is **not** subject to social/health insurance and (as sick pay) is exempt from income tax.

## 8. Srážky ze mzdy — garnishments (exekuce / insolvence)

Computed from **net wage**; the debtor keeps the **nezabavitelná částka** (non-attachable amount). The 2026 method changed: **normativní náklady na bydlení were abolished (1.10.2025)** and replaced by *normativní nájemné + energetický paušál* under the new zákon o dávce státní sociální pomoci.

- **[2026 confirmed] nezabavitelná částka na dlužníka (individual) = 14 101,50 Kč** = 85 % of (životní minimum jednotlivce + normativní nájemné 1 osoba + energetický paušál). A further per-dependent amount (¼ of that) applies.
- **Fully attachable remainder threshold** (above which the rest is taken without limit) = **1.9× the sum** = **31 521 Kč** [2026]. Between the non-attachable amount and this cap, the remainder is split into thirds (1st third — ordinary claims, 2nd third — preferential claims, 3rd third — always to debtor).
- The 2026 amounts are **frozen for the whole year** at their 1.1.2026 values.

Sources: https://www.podnikatel.cz/clanky/exekucni-srazky-v-roce-2026-skutecna-nezabavitelna-castka-bude-vyssi/ ; https://exekuce.justice.cz/vypocet-srazek-ze-mzdy/

## 9. Minimum wage & (public-sector) zaručený plat

- **[2026 confirmed] minimální mzda 22 400 Kč/měsíc, 134,40 Kč/hod** (40 h/week). Source: https://mpsv.gov.cz/minimalni-mzda-od-ledna-2026-vzroste-o-1-600-korun-na-22-400-korun-mesicne
- **[2025] 20 800 Kč/měsíc, 124,40 Kč/hod**. Source: https://www.kurzy.cz/mzda/minimalni-mzda-2025/
- **Valorisation:** from 2025 the minimum wage = coefficient × predicted average wage (2026 coefficient 0.434), set 2 years ahead by gov. regulation (no more annual ad-hoc decree). Source: https://mpsv.gov.cz/minimalni-mzda
- **Zaručená mzda abolished in the private sector from 2025**; only **zaručený plat** for the public/state sector remains, in **4 groups** [2026]: 22 400 / 26 880 / 31 360 / 35 840 Kč. Source: https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/zaruceny-plat-uz-plati-jen-pro-statni-a-verejnou-sferu-zvysuje-se-i-pro-rok-2026/
- **Payroll impact:** minimum wage is the **minimum health-insurance base** (§2) and the reference for the daňový bonus income tests (§4).
