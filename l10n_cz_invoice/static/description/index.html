CZ Invoice (statutory PDF)

Customer DIČ, payment symbols and the mandatory statutory phrases on the Czech invoice

The Czech country layer that adds the statutory invoice content to Odoo's invoice, on top of core and l10n_cz (which already prints the IČO and the trade-registry line). It supplies the customer DIČ, the Czech payment symbols and the mandatory statutory phrases — domestic reverse charge (§92a), intra-Community supply / exempt (§64) and export (§66) — on the printed invoice.

Key features

  • Prints the customer DIČ next to the IČO / DIČ DPH that the core CZ layer already shows (sourced from l10n_cssk_core).
  • Adds the Czech payment symbols to the invoice information row — variabilní symbol, konstantní symbol and specifický symbol.
  • Auto-detects and prints the mandatory statutory phrases: domestic reverse charge (§92a), intra-Community supply / exempt (§64) and export (§66).
  • A per-tax CZ reverse charge (§92a) flag drives the §92a phrase, with a distinct name so it does not collide with the EE l10n_cz_reports field (which is also honoured when that module is installed).
  • A free-text manual legal note printed in addition to any auto-detected phrase.
  • The phrase auto-detection (zero-rate + partner country + the per-tax §92a flag) is a best-effort heuristic — have an accountant confirm the mapping.

Usage

Install the module on a Czech company. On customer invoices the customer DIČ and any populated payment symbols appear automatically on the PDF. Tick CZ Reverse Charge phrase (§92a) on the relevant tax to print the §92a phrase; the §64 and §66 phrases are derived from the zero-rate taxes and the customer's country. Type any extra wording in Invoice Legal Note (manual) on the invoice to have it printed as well.
