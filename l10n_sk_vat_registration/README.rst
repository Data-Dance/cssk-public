SK VAT Registration Category (§ 4 / § 7 / § 7a)
=================================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**Records under which paragraph of the Slovak VAT Act a partner's IČ DPH was issued, and warns when a domestic reverse charge is aimed at a § 7 or § 7a registrant, who is not a platiteľ.**

What it is for
================

An IČ DPH does not mean the same thing for everyone who holds one. A Slovak business registered under **§ 7** (acquisition of goods from another member state) or **§ 7a** (services received from or supplied to another member state) gets a valid IČ DPH, which VIES confirms — but it is **not a platiteľ dane** (VAT payer). It does not charge VAT on domestic supplies, does not deduct input VAT, and the domestic reverse charge (*prenesenie daňovej povinnosti*) under § 69 ods. 12 cannot be applied to it.

VIES answers only valid / not valid. This module adds the registration paragraph to the contact and warns when an invoice applies the domestic reverse charge to a customer who is not a platiteľ.

Legal background
==================

All references are to the `zákon o DPH <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/>`__.

- `§ 4 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-4>`__ — a taxable person established in Slovakia becomes a **platiteľ**, for example after its turnover exceeds EUR 50,000 in a calendar year; `§ 4b <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-4b>`__ — group registration; `§ 4c <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-4c>`__ — group registration ex officio; `§ 5 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-5>`__ — a foreign person becomes a platiteľ by supplying in Slovakia.

- `§ 7 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-7>`__ — a person not obliged to register under § 4 or § 5 (or a non-taxable legal person) registers before its acquisitions of goods from other member states reach EUR 14,000 in a calendar year, or voluntarily.

- `§ 7a <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-7a>`__ — a taxable person not obliged to register under § 4 registers before receiving a service from a foreign person for which it must pay the tax, or before supplying a service whose place of supply is in another member state.

- `§ 69 ods. 12 <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/#paragraf-69.odsek-12>`__ — the domestic reverse charge applies when **both** the supplier and the customer are platitelia with an IČ DPH assigned under § 4, § 4b, § 4c or § 5. A § 7 / § 7a registrant is neither, so the supplier charges and pays Slovak VAT as usual.

Setting it up
===============

Nothing to configure. On the contact form, below the IČ DPH (*Tax ID*), two fields appear for Slovak partners and partners without a country:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - Meaning
   * - Druh registrácie DPH
     - the paragraph the IČ DPH was issued under: § 4, § 4b, § 4c, § 5, § 7 or § 7a; § 6 *zásielkový predaj* for a distance-selling registration made before 1 July 2021
   * - Platiteľ DPH od
     - the day the VAT registration took effect, per the Finančná správa record (shown once the category is set)

Fill them in by hand, or let ``partner_autocomplete_orsf_sk`` fill them from the register when you look a company up. Values the register writes in near-miss forms (``§4``, ``§ 7a``, ``7A``) are recognised; anything unrecognised leaves the field **empty**, never "not a platiteľ".

The warning on invoices
=========================

On a customer invoice of a Slovak company a yellow warning appears at the top, in Slovak with an English translation, when all of these hold:

- the customer's category is **§ 7** or **§ 7a**;

- the customer's country is Slovakia, or not set;

- a line carries a tax marked as a Slovak domestic reverse charge (the *SK Reverse Charge (§69/12)* flag of ``l10n_sk_invoice``).

When the invoice is posted the same text is written to its chatter. The warning **never blocks** posting: correct the taxes yourself — the supply is taxed with ordinary Slovak VAT.

Without ``l10n_sk_invoice`` there is no reverse charge flag on taxes, and the check stays silent.

Limitations
=============

- **The category is only as current as its last update.** Nothing refreshes it on a schedule; a customer who becomes a § 4 platiteľ keeps its old category until you or the register lookup change it.

- **Only the domestic reverse charge is checked.** Other consequences of a § 7 / § 7a registration (for example on intra-Community supplies or on the kontrolný výkaz) are not.

- **The § 6 option is historical.** It stands for the distance-selling registration of § 6 as worded until 30 June 2021, whose holders were platitelia, and counts as one. The § 6 in force today is the platiteľ's duty to notify its bank accounts and registers nobody, so a current register record should never carry it.

- **Platiteľ DPH od** is the registration date for every category, including § 7 / § 7a registrants, who are not platitelia.

- Vendor bills are not checked.
