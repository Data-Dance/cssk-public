=======================================
Received Advance Invoices — Inventory
=======================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**A received advance invoice never creates a receipt; the goods are received on the purchase order they were ordered on.**

A received advance invoice of ``purchase_order_advance_invoice`` is a purchase order of its own, and paying it confirms it. With Inventory installed, Odoo would then create a receipt for its stockable products, and the same goods would be expected twice: on the advance and on the purchase order they were ordered on.

With this add-on an advance never creates a receipt — not when it is paid, not when it is confirmed by hand, and not when a line is added or a quantity raised afterwards. Receive the goods on the **parent purchase order**, as usual. Ordinary purchase orders are not affected.

It installs itself when ``purchase_order_advance_invoice`` and Inventory (``purchase_stock``) are both installed. There is nothing to set up.
