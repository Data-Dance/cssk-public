==============================================
Attendance Kiosk: Reason for Leaving (CZ/SK)
==============================================

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-AGPL--3-blue.png
   :alt: License: AGPL-3

**The attendance kiosk asks why an employee is leaving and turns the answer into a draft time off for HR to approve.**

What it is for
================

Out of the box the attendance kiosk only checks people in and out. In Czech and Slovak workplaces an employee who leaves during the day usually says why: a visit to the doctor, accompanying a family member, an errand, an office visit. This module asks that question at the kiosk. When an employee checks **out**, the kiosk shows a **Work ended** button and one button per reason. Work ended is an ordinary check-out. A reason records the check-out, stores the reason on the attendance, and creates a **draft time off** (*To Approve*) from the check-out time to the end of the working day, for HR to approve.

It depends only on Odoo's *Attendances* and *Time Off*, and works with either payroll engine or without payroll.

Legal background
==================

The usual reasons are the personal obstacles to work in the labour codes:

- **Czech Republic.** Examination or treatment at a doctor and accompanying a family member are obstacles on the employee's side under `§ 199 zákoníku práce <https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2006/262>`__; their scope and whether the wage is compensated are set by nařízení vlády č. 590/2006 Sb. (annex, points 1 and 8).

- **Slovakia.** Examination or treatment in a medical facility and accompanying a family member are important personal obstacles under `§ 141 ods. 2 písm. a) and b) Zákonníka práce <https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2001/311/#paragraf-141.odsek-2>`__; paid time off for one's own examination is limited to seven days a year.

The module records the time away and leaves the judgement to HR: whether the absence is justified, and whether it is paid, is decided when the draft is approved and by the time off type it carries.

Setting it up
===============

The module creates no time off types of its own: the types, and what each pays, belong to the national payroll modules or to you. The Czech payroll modules ship *Paid Obstacle - Doctor Visit / Escort*; the Slovak ones ship *Paid Obstacle — Medical Examination* and *Paid Obstacle — Accompanying a Family Member*, each with its seven-day yearly quota. Offer a type at the kiosk by ticking **Show in Attendance Kiosk** on it (*Time Off › Configuration › Time Off Types*). The flagged types are listed under **Time Off › Configuration › Kiosk Reasons**, where you drag them into order, give each a colour for its button, and add new ones. Recommended for a kiosk reason:

- **Duration** in hours, since a departure is part of a day;

- **Requires allocation** off: with it on, an employee without an allocation gets no draft time off (the attendance is still tagged), and the form warns about it;

- an **approval** step, so the draft stays *To Approve*. With no approval the time off is validated at once.

The kiosk itself is set up as usual under *Attendances › Configuration › Settings*. With no reason flagged, it behaves exactly as without the module.

At the kiosk
==============

1. The employee scans a badge, or picks their name and enters the PIN.

2. If they are about to check out, the reason screen appears.

3. **Work ended** checks them out normally; a reason checks them out and creates the draft time off.

**Returning the same day.** When the employee checks in again the same day, the draft is shortened to end at the return, so it covers only the time away. Each departure gets its own draft. Time off HR has already approved is never changed.

**After an automatic check-out.** If Odoo's *Automatic Check Out* closed a forgotten attendance, the next scan asks for the reason of that earlier departure and says when the automatic check-out happened. A reason tags that attendance and creates its draft; the default just checks the employee in. The real departure time is unknown here, so HR corrects the hours on approval.

HR finds the drafts among ordinary time off requests (*Time Off › Management*). The reason is shown on each attendance and can be grouped by (*Departure Reason*).

Limits
========

- Shortening the draft on return works for a kiosk check-in only, not for a check-in from the backend.

- A departure entirely outside working hours gets a one-hour draft that counts no working hours; HR adjusts it.

- With *Absence Management* on, only approved time off reduces the expected hours, so approve the drafts promptly or the departures show as a shortfall in the meantime.

- In a multi-company database the draft belongs to the kiosk's company; an approver sees it only with that company active.
